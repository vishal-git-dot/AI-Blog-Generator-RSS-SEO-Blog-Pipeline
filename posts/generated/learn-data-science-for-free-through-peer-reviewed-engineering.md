# Learn Data Science for Free Through Peer-Reviewed Engineering

## Overview

Data Science is often taught in a vacuum—clean datasets, perfect algorithms, and isolated notebooks. The real world is messy. The Data Science department at Dokkho Tech prepares learners for the reality of data engineering and analysis. The Curriculum Data Wrangling: Dealing with imperfect, real-world data. Statistical Modeling: Understanding the math behind the algorithms. Machine Learning Pipelines: Deploying models into production environments. Because Dokkho is a peer-reviewed ecosystem, learners must explain their models and defend their analytical choices to their peers, simulating a real data team environment.
