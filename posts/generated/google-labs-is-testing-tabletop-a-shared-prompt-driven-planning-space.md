# Google Labs is testing Tabletop, a shared prompt-driven planning space

## Overview

Google Labs is testing Tabletop, a shared prompt-driven planning space Google Labs is testing Tabletop, an experiment that turns prompts and templates into shared interactive workspaces. The supported examples include: Trip itineraries Dinner polls Expense splitting Tournament brackets A workspace can be shared with collaborators, who can add links, files and ideas while the plan changes in real time. Constraints to keep in mind Tabletop is not presented as a finished general-purpose collaboration platform. Access is rolling out through a U.S. waitlist for people 18 and older. The available roles, integrations and generated controls need to be checked inside the experiment. For an important workflow, test the invite behavior and permissions first rather than assuming the generated workspace has the controls you need.
