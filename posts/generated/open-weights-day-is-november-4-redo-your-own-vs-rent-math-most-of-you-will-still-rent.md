Which makes own-vs-rent a recheckable question — not a stance you adopted once and forgot. A downloadable model is not a free model Open weights mean you get the recipe, not the meal. Once the weights are in hand, every cost the vendor was absorbing becomes yours: Hardware. A 2.8-trillion-parameter model does not run on the office laptop. It needs serious GPUs — bought, or rented by the hour. Operations. Someone installs it, keeps it running, restarts it when it falls over, and upgrades it when a better version ships. That someone is you, or a contractor you pay. Security and hygiene. The server is now your server — patching, access control, backups. A model with access to your business data is an attack surface like any other. When you rent AI, you are borrowing a vendor's ops team amortized across thousands of customers. That is the actual product. The model weights were never the expensive part. The honest checklist: when self-hosting still does not make sense Run down this list before you get excited about November 4: No compliance driver. If no regulator, contract, or client requires your data to stay on your infrastructure, you would be self-hosting to save money that probably is not there. Unpredictable volume. Self-hosting pays for peak capacity whether or not the peak shows up. Bursty, occasional use is exactly what APIs were built for. No ops person. Not "you are handy with computers" — an actual human accountable for uptime. If the honest answer is "it would be me, at night," that is the answer. Most small businesses check all three boxes. That is not a failure — it is the correct result, and knowing it is the point of the exercise. The three signals it is time to reprice Self-hosting earns a fresh look when any of these is true: Regulated or confidential data. Healthcare, legal, finance, or client contracts that demand data residency. Here the comparison is not price — it is what a vendor's breach clause costs you versus keeping the data inside in the first place. Predictable high volume. Steady, heavy, round-the-clock token use flattens your unit cost and makes capacity planning real. Volume you can forecast is capacity you can buy efficiently. Renegotiation leverage. A tested, working fallback is the only thing that makes a vendor's renewal desk polite. Even if you never switch, the credible ability to switch reprices the contract you are already in — which matters in a market where frontier AI is being financed on expectations and the bill eventually lands somewhere.
