What is Termux? If you're unfamiliar, Termux is essentially a Linux environment running on your Android device. It’s not root access, but it provides a surprisingly comprehensive suite of tools: a package manager ( pkg ), bash scripting, Python, Node.js, and more. Think of it as a pocket-sized server. It’s available on F-Droid and the Google Play Store, though F-Droid is generally preferred for its open-source ethos.
