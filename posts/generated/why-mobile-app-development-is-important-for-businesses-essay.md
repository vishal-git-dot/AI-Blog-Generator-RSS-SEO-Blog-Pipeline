# Why Mobile App Development is Important for Businesses Essay

## Overview

Nowadays, people use their smartphone devices to perform all types of actions from searching for information to ordering food or a movie. The importance of mobile applications in people’s daily lives has changed how people find what they need – and how businesses must provide what people require. A mobile app can become a vital part of any company, connecting it with its clients. It can simplify and speed up day-to-day operations, as well as facilitate communication with customers. However, the development of such an application has specific and special mobile application development requirements. Define the Goal of the Application First of all, before proceeding to the actual development of the application under the auspices of a mobile application development company, the main goal of the application, as well as the problems it will solve, should be identified. An application can help to sell a product or provide a service, for example, to order a table at a restaurant. The application can provide assistance in carrying out day-to-day operations or serve as a tool for interaction with the client. A well-defined goal will allow identifying the necessary features of the application that will ultimately make the users’ work easier. At the same time, the presence of unnecessary ones will only complicate the process of working with the application. Focus on the User Experience One of the common mistakes made by companies when launching an app is the emphasis on the appearance of the application. Although the graphical interface is indeed essential, one must remember that an application is intended to solve specific problems for a user rather than just look attractive. In this regard, two more concepts should be introduced: UX and UI design. The first term refers to the overall feel and interaction with the application. The second term refers to the specific graphic elements. CodeXmattrix specializes in human-centered mobile app UI/UX development, including graphic design, responsive and intuitive interfaces, and user-centered approaches to mobile application creation. A multi-platform approach to navigation, the visibility and convenience of the buttons, the clarity of the accompanying text, the presence of guides or instructions will ensure the quality of your application. Pay Attention to Performance No one wants to wait for an application to load or start working properly. That is why performance should be paramount when creating applications. The application should be responsive, fast, and reliable in terms of technology, since it is designed to make life easier and be used frequently. The application should be developed in such a way that it performs the required functions quickly and efficiently on all types of devices, operating systems, and connection types. An excellent technical solution is not enough if a user cannot intuitively navigate the site or work with it without confusion. Select an Appropriate Tech Stack Another requirement for creating an application is selecting the technology stack. Companies should choose which technologies they will use for mobile app development. Native mobile application development is an ideal choice for iOS and Android devices. However, in other cases, it is better to draw attention to such cross-platform technologies as React Native or Flutter for mobile application development. CodeXmattrix offers solutions in native mobile development for iOS and Android. Any technology stack selection should be guided by the company’s specific mobile application development requirements and not only by technology. Pay Attention to the Security and Safety of the Application During the operation of a mobile phone, users store various personal data on their smartphones. However, an application may contain even more valuable information, such as details of a bank account or other secret data of the user or company. In this regard, the security and safety of the application should also be considered during its development. CodeXmattrix offers to pay attention to the security of the application: securing the application from external attacks, protecting the data stored in it, implementing appropriate permission settings, and other security features. Updating applications is another factor in long-term mobile application development. Technology, and especially smartphones, is changing so rapidly that sometimes it is challenging to keep up with innovations. Conduct a Thorough Testing Testing the application is an essential part of the application development process. If a program runs perfectly on a programmer’s phone, it does not necessarily mean that it will work correctly on all other devices. Applications need to be tested for ease of use, speed, and security. Sometimes it can be helpful to gather feedback from real users. There may be cases where a specific feature is confusing for a person because they do not understand how it works. However, from the perspective of programming, this option is fully justified. The opinions of real users of the product can significantly help in determining the MVP of the product. Strengthen the Relationship with Users Through the App One of the essential components of the application is the relationship with the user. There are several reasons why a company needs to have an application on a smartphone. Some businesses replace their website with an application. Other applications supplement companies’ work and provide the ability to perform routine operations independently. In each case, it is necessary to remember that the app serves to make the work of the users of this application as convenient as possible. The users of the application can customize it to their liking or receive notifications and reminders about events. A company needs to take care of maintaining long-term relationships with all users of the product. An application is also an essential tool for providing customers with a wide range of services: from storing personal information and loyalty cards to providing discounts or special offers. As a result, it should be said that there is no sense in creating an application just because other companies have one. Instead, one should strive to create a product that users will be glad to use often. Plan the Further Existence and Development of the Program The actual creation and delivery of the application is not the end of the work. After the program has been released, it is necessary to collect statistics about it, process queries and complaints, study the features of its functioning, and correct errors. Creating documentation, providing a description of the application and instructions for use, is also a necessary activity. Further development and improvements should be made based on the collected user feedback. Sometimes even the most carefully crafted apps need adjustments and improvements. The most important part is determining what technology to use and defining the basic principles of the application. In addition, the company’s requirements for the operating systems, connection types, and devices should also be taken into account. The development of the application also depends on the planned update policy, the collection and analysis of user statistics, and other factors. This is especially crucial for applications that users rely on for performing daily operations or those that are used frequently. The program must be constantly maintained, errors fixed, and new features added. Concluding Thoughts Although some companies do not see the need for a mobile application, it is a powerful tool that can provide essential services. However, an application must be created with a specific goal in mind. An application should make life easier, be intuitive, and at the same time secure. The initial step in developing an application is the selection of the main goal, based on which the required features will be selected. Equally important is the study of the user experience and the technologies that will be used in the product.
