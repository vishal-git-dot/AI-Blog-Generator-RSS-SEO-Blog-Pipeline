# Mastering Video Hooks with PAS Framework

## Overview

Retention dropping in the first 30 seconds? You're not alone. Most tech creators dump benchmark charts, patch notes, or generic intros right at the start. The fix isn't a flashier edit—it’s structured storytelling. Enter the PAS Framework : P roblem, A gitate, S olve. It’s a simple copywriting formula that hooks tech-minded viewers instantly by tapping directly into their frustrations. 1. Problem Identify the exact pain point your viewer is experiencing. Skip the channel intro and state the issue immediately. 2. Agitate Rub salt in the wound. Make them feel the annoyance, wasted money, or lost productivity caused by that problem. 3. Solve Position your video as the relief. Deliver the fix they’ve been searching for. PAS in Action (Tech Channel Example) Instead of starting with: "Hey guys, today we're looking at memory management on Apple Silicon..." Try this: Problem: "Is your 8GB Mac constantly freezing the second you open Docker and Chrome together?" Agitate: "You dropped over a grand on a 'Pro' machine, but you're still staring at spinning beachballs, losing unsaved code, and missing deadlines while your swap memory pegs out." Solve: "In this video, I’m sharing three hidden terminal tweaks that immediately free up 4GB of RAM—no upgrades required." Why It Works for Tech YouTubers Tech audiences want fast, practical solutions. By agitating the problem, you build tension and establish instant relevance. When you present the solution, staying to watch the video becomes a no-brainer. Apply PAS to your next video hook or script outline. Your retention graph will thank you.
