# I built an AI "Second Brain" for my 10,000+ screenshots 🧠📸

## Overview

I have a problem: I'm a visual hoarder. My camera roll is a 10,000-image digital graveyard where information goes to die. WiFi passwords, code snippets, and flight credits—all lost. I built Skreenly ( https://skreenly.app ) to solve this. It’s an AI screenshot inbox that reads every image (GPT-4o), auto-categorizes them, and makes them searchable by content. I'm an indie dev building this to help with my own ADHD object-permanence issues. I just launched 6 free web tools to demo the vision: https://skreenly.app/ocr I'd love to hear how other builders manage their visual capture mess!
