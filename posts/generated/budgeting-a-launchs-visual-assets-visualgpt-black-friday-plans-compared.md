One exported asset may follow several candidate generations, and a change to the launch message can send the whole set back for revision. A useful asset backlog names the placement, dimensions, source material, and acceptance criteria for each item. A homepage hero needs a different composition from a social preview; a product image needs accurate details even when the surrounding scene changes. For a hypothetical launch, list one hero, three social cards, and two product illustrations. Those six deliverables do not imply six generations.
