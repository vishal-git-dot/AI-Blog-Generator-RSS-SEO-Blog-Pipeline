# DR-AP5322 — Flexible Wi-Fi 7 Connectivity for Next-Generation Networks

## Overview

Built around the Qualcomm IPQ5322 platform, the DR-AP5322 is a high-performance Wi-Fi 7 AP Router designed for ISPs, WISPs, system integrators, and customized wireless networking projects. With support for QCN9274/QCN6274 Wi-Fi 7 modules, the platform provides a flexible foundation for next-generation wireless deployments, while its 4 × 2.5GbE Ethernet ports and 1 × 10G SFP port provide high-speed wired connectivity for demanding network environments. The aluminum enclosure combines durability with a professional industrial design, making the DR-AP5322 suitable for commercial Wi-Fi, broadband access, enterprise networking, and customized OEM/ODM solutions.
