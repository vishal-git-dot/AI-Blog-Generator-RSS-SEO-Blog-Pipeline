# How to Trim Audio for a Lollapalooza Video — Free, in Your Browser

## Overview

A Lollapalooza set recording is full of great moments — and a lot of crowd noise, long gaps between songs, and a slow build-up before the band starts playing. If you want a short clip you can actually share, trim it down first. AudioCutter is a free online audio cutter that runs entirely in your browser: no install, no upload, no account. Step 1: Open AudioCutter in your browser and drop in the recording. The file stays on your device and the waveform appears right away, so you can see the loud parts and the quiet gaps at a glance. Step 2: Drag the handles to keep the best part. Cut the crowd chatter at the start, trim the dead air between songs, and keep the chorus or the solo that makes the clip worth sharing. Step 3: Add a short fade in and fade out. A one-second fade at each end makes the clip start and end cleanly instead of cutting off mid-note, which matters a lot when the clip goes into a video. Step 4: Export as MP3, play the clip back once, and drop it into your video editor. If a section still feels long, go back and trim it again — the whole round trip takes about a minute. Keep the original recording untouched and work on a copy, so you can always go back for a different moment from the same set. Short, clean clips get watched; long raw recordings do not. Disclosure: I built AudioCutter, a free browser-based audio cutter. Try it at https://audiocutter.me/ — no install, no upload, no account needed.
