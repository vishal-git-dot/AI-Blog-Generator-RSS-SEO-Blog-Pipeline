# Orbura: a camera check-in that becomes a baseline — Presage SmartSpectra SDK Challenge

## Overview

This is a submission for the Presage SmartSpectra SDK Challenge What I Built Orbura Companion — a consent-first camera check-in that turns the Presage SmartSpectra SDK into an about-a-minute wellness ritual, wired directly into Orbura , a longitudinal health platform that converts physiological signals into one safe action . The product architecture is split across two complimentary surfaces: Orbura Companion (Electron + @smartspectra/node-sdk at showcases/presage-smartspectra/ ) is the capture instrument . It owns the local camera stream, the explicit consent gate, the guided scan, and the live multi-channel physiological waveforms. Orbura Web Platform (Next.js at orbura.famile.xyz ) is the canonical record . Check-ins land securely as camera_vitals observations alongside wearable data, feeding autonomic recovery baselines, exception triage, and clinical continuity. The core thesis: A single reading is trivia — accumulation is health. Day 1 is a reading; Day 30 is a baseline. The Video Showcase Watch on YouTube (85-second film with full spatial cinematography, narration, and physiological research callouts) . The film demonstrates the complete loop from first principles: The Human Moment: Daily check-in at the kitchen table. On-Device Consent: Explicit privacy guarantee before camera hardware initializes. The Sensor Lock: Remote optical plethysmography (rPPG) locking onto micro-capillary vascular flushes across facial landmarks. Live Signal Extraction: Real-time extraction of autonomic Respiratory Sinus Arrhythmia (RSA) into four live charts. The Payoff: 9 BR/min resolving into "Today's one action: Take ten slow breaths before your next meal." Longitudinal Record: 30 days of breathing waves tightening into an empirical personal recovery baseline. Scale of Opportunity: Over 3.8 billion existing cameras in pockets and laptops transformed into proactive health sensors. Visual Architecture & Live Product Flow 1. The On-Device Privacy Architecture Before a single video frame is captured, the user is presented with a clear privacy contract. Processing is performed in-memory on the user's Mac using the SmartSpectra SDK. Raw pixels, video, and facial landmarks are never stored or transmitted. The camera indicator remains visible, backed by a persistent "Live · Not recording" status. 2. Multi-Channel Signal Extraction (rPPG & RSA) Upon starting measurement, the SmartSpectra SDK initiates remote photoplethysmography. The interface visualizes four continuous physiological channels: Breathing Rate (BR/min) Raw Breathing Waveform (Respiratory Sinus Arrhythmia) Pulse Rate (BPM) Arterial Pressure Trace Confidence and position metrics continuously guide the user through a dynamic SVG guide oval, turning vibrant green ( Ok — Hold still and record ) once optical lock is achieved. 3. The Payoff: One Manageable Action Most health dashboards dump endless charts on users, creating cognitive overload and anxiety. Orbura deliberately reduces the session to: Deterministic Vitals: Measured breathing rate and pulse. One Thing to Notice: An educational takeaway on vagal autonomic recovery. Today's One Action: A single, clinic-approved behavioral micro-step that fits into the user's day. 4. The Longitudinal Record (Day 1 → Day 30) When the user taps Save to Orbura , only numeric values and timestamps cross the boundary via an encrypted link token ( POST /api/vitals/check-in ). Over weeks, these check-ins establish an empirical baseline band. Deviations trigger proactive nudges or clinician review before minor stress compounds into chronic exhaustion. Most Creative or Delightful Use Check-in as Ritual: We designed the interaction as a quiet, respectful morning ritual rather than clinical telemetry. Best Thoughtful Real-World Use GLP-1 Care Continuity: Designed to solve a massive real-world healthcare bottleneck: helping patients navigating chronic medication titration stay engaged without needing expensive, charged wearable hardware. Uncompromising Privacy: Real on-device processing. No raw images, no video recordings, and no facial landmark arrays ever touch a server or cloud bucket. Honest Feedback at Every Boundary: Thoughtful fallback states across camera permission denials, unlinked accounts, and variable lighting. Consent and Data Handling Disclosure Explicit Consent Gate: The camera stream is blocked until the user accepts the transparent privacy charter. On-Device rPPG: Photoplethysmography computation executes locally in-memory via the SmartSpectra SDK. Numbers-Only Sync: Only scalar metrics (breathing rate, pulse, HRV timestamp) leave the machine, and only upon explicit user tap on Save to Orbura . Wellness Scope: All outputs carry the SmartSpectra wellness disclaimer — intended strictly for wellness tracking, stress recovery, and habits, never clinical diagnosis or treatment.
