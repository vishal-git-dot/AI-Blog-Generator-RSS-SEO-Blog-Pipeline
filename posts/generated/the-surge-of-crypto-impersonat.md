How Impersonation Scams Operate Impersonation scams typically utilize a combination of social engineering and AI-driven technologies to create convincing fraudulent identities. The common techniques include: Deepfake Videos : Using AI-generated synthetic media to replicate the appearance and voice of trusted individuals.
