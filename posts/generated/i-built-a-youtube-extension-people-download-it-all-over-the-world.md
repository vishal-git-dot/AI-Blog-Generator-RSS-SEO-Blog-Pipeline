# I Built a YouTube Extension. People Download It All Over the World.

## Overview

When I was just starting out, I kept asking myself: “Why bother? There’s already plenty out there — ChatGPT, websites, other extensions.” But I got tired of spending half an hour on videos I could understand in a minute. That’s how the whole thing started. What Got on My Nerves You open YouTube and there’s a 40–60 minute video. An interview, a tutorial, a review. No time to watch the whole thing, but you don’t want to miss it either — what if the one important idea is in there? I tried different options: Websites — they ask you to sign up, then hit you with “limit reached, pay up” ChatGPT — you copy the link, explain what you need, wait. A few minutes per video Other extensions — some only work on articles, some only on YouTube, some spit out a mess instead of a clear summary At some point I just shelved the project and thought: “Okay, enough.” Why I Finished It Anyway Everything Right on YouTube I didn’t need “another website.” I needed this: you’re on YouTube → you click the button → you get the gist. That’s what the extension does: Short summary — main points as a list, no fluff Full video text — if subtitles exist, you can read and copy them Alt+S shortcut — works on other sites too No jumping to another tab, no copying links by hand. Not Just YouTube Then I realized the same problem shows up with articles and long text online. So the extension can also: summarize articles on websites work with PDFs in the browser summarize selected text — highlight it, right-click ask questions about a video or article (“what’s this about?”, “what should I remember?”) translate the summary into another language save history — go back to an old summary anytime Used in Countries All Over the World I built it for myself. I thought it would stay a small tool “for me and people like me.” After I published it on the Chrome Web Store, installs started coming from all over — the US, Europe, Asia, Latin America. No ads: people just Google things like “youtube summary” or “summarize youtube video” and find it. 🔥 YouTube Video Summarizer: Free with AI — https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/youtube-video-summarizer/pgodlmoknkhodghicfjchampnbdajjno Turns out “no time to watch a long video” isn’t just my problem — it’s everyone’s. Why I Wrote This Not to say “I have the best AI on the planet.” Because I got tired of choosing between “watch for an hour” and “walk away with nothing.” If you open YouTube, see a long video, and think “I wish someone would just tell me what’s in it” — this is for that. Try it on any long video with subtitles. Usually half a minute is enough to decide — keep watching or close the tab. youtube #chromeextension #productivity #ai #summarizer
