# Thank you for the credits: every grant behind Slid Phi Labs

## Overview

Slid Phi Labs is a small lab with big compute bills. Everything we ship — the Agent Commerce Network, TeachAid, the Rider identity system, our hackathon entries — runs on infrastructure that someone else helped pay for. This post is our public thank-you, one grantor at a time, with exactly what each grant powers. LiveKit — $17,000 in credits + one year of Scale LiveKit approved our team for $17k in credits plus a year of Scale. That is our entire real-time voice and video story: the TeachAid spoken classroom, live agent demos, and every WebRTC session we run for testing. Without it, our voice work would be slides instead of software. Cartesia — a 12-month grant (96M characters/year + $300/mo agent credits) Cartesia granted us a year of best-in-class text-to-speech: 96M characters a year plus monthly agent credits. Every spoken reply in our demos — the teacher's voice, the hired agent's voice — is a Cartesia voice. Latency you can talk over changes what agents feel like. Tavily — 10,000 add-on credits Tavily put 10,000 search credits on our workspace (on top of the Researcher plan). As of this week, Tavily is the default search provider across our agentic surfaces: our MCP spl_research tool and every research-shaped hired-agent run ground themselves with fresh Tavily sources. Agents that can't search are guessing; ours don't have to. Nebius — Token Factory credit + the AI Builder Program Nebius welcomed us with Token Factory credit and the AI Builder Program, and our NVIDIA Nemotron builds run through it. Our Rider Marketplace demo — hire a verified agent, Nemotron does the work — executes against Nebius-hosted models. Builder programs that actually include inference credit are rare, and this one does. LangSmith — $100 via the Nebius partner shelf One hundred dollars of LangSmith credit, which goes straight into tracing and evaluating our agent runs: what the model saw, what it called, where the time went. Measured agents improve; unmeasured ones just cost more. Toloka — $50 via the Nebius partner shelf Fifty dollars of Toloka credit, earmarked for the human-data side: labeling and evaluation tasks where models grading models isn't good enough. Tandem — $50 via the Nebius partner shelf Fifty dollars of Tandem credit, earmarked alongside the rest of the partner shelf as our data and eval needs land. Why post this Grants to small labs are leverage: a few thousand dollars of credits turns into public software, open evals, and hackathon entries that everyone can inspect. If you run a credits program and you want your logo on a wall like this one, you know where to find us: corey@slidphilabs.com . And if you gave us credits — thank you. We spend them in public. — Corey & the lab, Slid Phi Labs
