# Anthropic Launches Claude Sheets, Whiteboards and Meetings — Claude Becomes a Full Workspace

## Overview

Claude is no longer just a chat window. Anthropic has launched Claude Sheets and Claude Whiteboards in beta on paid plans, made Claude Data generally available, shown off Claude Meetings for Team and Enterprise, and upgraded its interactive connectors. The pitch is plain: your whole working day, inside one app. Sheets and Whiteboards first The two headline additions arrive in beta on paid plans, across web, desktop and mobile. Claude Sheets brings spreadsheets into the conversation — ask Claude to build one and it will, formulas included. It has a 100,000-cell ceiling and 144 functions at launch, which is plenty for the models most people build day to day. Whiteboards, meanwhile, give Claude a shared surface to think on: drop ideas on a canvas, move them around, and watch the model work alongside you. Meetings, with a caveat Claude Meetings is the one to read carefully. Anthropic showed it off for Team and Enterprise customers, with Google Meet support at launch — Claude joins the call, takes notes and updates documents live as the meeting runs. It's still in beta, though, and the framing is "coming soon" rather than fully live. The idea is clear enough: the meeting notes write themselves, and the follow-up document is already half done by the time the call ends. The connectors get bigger The other piece is connectivity. Interactive connectors now let apps like Adobe, Atlassian, Box, Notion and HubSpot open in full beside a Claude conversation, so the work — and the context — never leaves the window. Anthropic says more than 9,000 plugins are now listed in the Claude Marketplace. Read the full announcements on Anthropic's news page or head to Claude to try the new tools. Anthropic has been shipping fast lately — Claude Fable 5.2 landed just days ago, and the contest with OpenAI's Businesses in ChatGPT is now a head-on race. The question isn't whether assistants become workspaces — it's whether workers want their AI sitting in every meeting. That one is still open. Originally published on Rohan Tech Official .
