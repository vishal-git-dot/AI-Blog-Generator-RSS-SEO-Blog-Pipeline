# PhotoFig

## Overview

Website URL: https://photofig.com Tagline: The product photo studio for serious sellers. About US:(PhotoFig) PhotoFig turns everyday product photos into consistent, store-ready catalog images without a studio or a designer. Every upload gets automatic background removal. From there, place the product in an AI-generated scene, picked from suggested themes or written as your own prompt, add shadows that sit it naturally in that scene, clean up stray marks with Magic Eraser, and upscale small photos. OpenAI's GPT-Image-2.5 models make precise, prompt-driven edits to the product itself. A full canvas editor handles layout, text and brand styling. Catalog work is where PhotoFig differs from one-photo-at-a-time tools. Save any finished look as a template and run it over hundreds of photos in a batch. Catalog Resize produces every size a store needs from the same images. Results published directly to WooCommerce, and Pro and Studio plans include a REST API with signed webhooks for automating the whole pipeline. PhotoFig is available in English, German, French, Italian and Lithuanian. Who it is for: small and growing online shops, marketplace sellers, and agencies managing product catalogs for clients. Pricing details: Free plan: 30 new photos a month, 15 AI credits a month, no card, no time limit. Starter: $9.95/month or $90/year (600 AI credits/month, 1,000 photos). Pro: $29.95/month or $270/year (2,000 AI credits/month, 5,000 photos, API access). Studio: $79/month or $708/year (7,500 AI credits/month, 20,000 photos, API access).
