Yes, but you need to understand the risks. The primary risk is volatility. If Bitcoin's price drops sharply, the lender will issue a margin call. This means you must add more crypto to your collateral pool. If you cannot, the platform will liquidate your collateral to repay the loan. To stay safe, borrow a lower percentage of your holdings.
