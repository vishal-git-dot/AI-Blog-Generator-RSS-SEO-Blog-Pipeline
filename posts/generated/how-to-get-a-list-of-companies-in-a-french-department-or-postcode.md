# How to Get a List of Companies in a French Department or Postcode

## Overview

You want a list of companies in a French department: every business in Lozère, in Paris, or in one postcode, as a spreadsheet. For single lookups by SIREN or SIRET, see the SIREN lookup API tutorial ; for lists by activity, see the companion guide on French companies by NAF code . This page covers lists by place with France Company Search (SIRENE) , an Apify Actor, with no API key. Location search has two traps, and our real runs show both. Department codes in 10 seconds: mainland departments use two characters, for example 48 (Lozère) or 75 (Paris). Corsica uses 2A and 2B . Overseas departments use 971 , 972 , 973 , 974 and 976 (there is no department 975 ). See INSEE's geographic code list for 2026 (in French). Postcodes have 5 digits, and one postcode can cover several communes: according to La Poste's postcode database , 48100 covers 15 communes, including Marvejols. Step-by-step: companies in one department Open France Company Search (SIRENE) , click Try for free and sign in. Clear the pre-filled SIREN numbers. In Departments ( departments ), add one or more codes, for example ["48"] . Optional: add NAF codes ( nafCodes , current NAF rév. 2 codes such as 62.01Z ) to narrow by activity, and set Status ( status ) to "active" . The default is "any" . Set Max companies ( maxItems , 1 to 10,000) to cap how many companies are output and charged. Click Start , then open the dataset and click Export for CSV or JSON. Which address matches? Head office vs establishments A French company (legal unit, SIREN) can have several establishments (sites, SIRET). The department and postcode filters match any establishment , not only the head office (siège). matchedEstablishmentSirets lists the sites that matched. In a live run on 15 October 2026 at 16:07 (UTC+8) with {"nafCodes": ["62.01Z"], "departments": ["48"], "maxItems": 5} , 2 of the 5 companies had their head office in another department and matched through an establishment in Lozère. Both are sole traders, so they are not shown. In other words, a "Lozère list" also includes companies that only have a site there. The other 3 have their head office in department 48. Here is one, shortened, with the street address removed: { "siren" : "892371139" , "name" : "SOLARIS BI" , "isIndividualEntrepreneur" : false , "headOffice" : { "siret" : "89237113900015" , "city" : "MARVEJOLS" , "department" : "48" }, "matchedEstablishmentSirets" : [ "89237113900015" , "89237113900023" ] } Both of its establishments matched, including the head office itself ( 89237113900015 ). To keep only companies based in the area, filter on the head office after the run. Load the JSON export and flatten headOffice ; codes stay strings, so leading zeros and 2A survive: import json import pandas as pd with open ( " dataset.json " , encoding = " utf-8 " ) as f : df = pd . json_normalize ( json . load ( f )) based = df [ df [ " headOffice.department " ] == " 48 " ] # or: df["headOffice.postalCode"] == "48100" On the run above, this keeps 3 of 5 rows. In a CSV export, Apify names nested columns with a slash ( headOffice/department ). Read it with pd.read_csv("dataset.csv", dtype=str) , or "48" becomes the number 48 and the filter keeps 0 rows. Step-by-step: companies in one postcode Use Postal codes ( postalCodes ) instead of departments. It takes 5-digit codes and, like departments, matches any establishment. If you fill in both, each department is combined with each postcode, so one of the two is usually enough.
