# NEXORA — Beyond the Frame: a cinematic skin for Video.js v10

## Overview

I built NEXORA — Beyond the Frame , a cinematic dark-glass skin for Video.js v10, and entered it in the Video.js Skins Design Challenge by Mux. What it does differently Aurora-gradient timeline with chapter markers and storyboard thumbnail previews while you scrub Glass control dock and menus that stay legible over bright and dark footage Full keyboard operation , visible focus, a live status announcer, and reduced-motion support Responsive layout that adapts from desktop to a thumb-friendly mobile dock Under the hood NEXORA is built on the real Video.js v10 HTML renderer ( @videojs/html ) with the Mux video component ( @videojs/mux-video ). The demo runs against a Mux-hosted video with chapters and English/Japanese subtitle tracks. The skin passed an axe-core accessibility audit with zero violations, and every control is a real Video.js component — nothing is a painted mockup. The project is open source under the Apache-2.0 license, with architecture, customization and accessibility documentation in the repo.
