This is a submission for the Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge Week 4: Ghost in the Machine What I Built Across every culture, autumn evenings and Dia de los Muertos gatherings are when families and friends sit around candlelit tables or crackling campfires to pass down folklore, ancestral lullabies, and cherished stories. Yet in the digital age, oral storytelling often gets flattened into static walls of text. LanternLoom AI ( lantern-loom-ai ) is an open-source, offline-first Interactive Voice, 60 FPS Animated Canvas & Procedural Audio Folklore Storybook Studio powered by Google Gemma 4 ( gemma4:26b-a4b & gemma4:e4b ) , Google Agent Development Kit (ADK) , Prior Labs TabPFN v2 , and Sentry Agent Tracing (OpenTelemetry) . Procedural WebAudio & 22.05kHz PCM WAV Soundscape Synthesizer ( audio_synth.py ) : Synthesizes polyphonic marimba chimes, crackling hearth harmonics, night-wind flutes, and celestial harp arpeggios in pure math, paired with live WebSpeech Voice Narration .
