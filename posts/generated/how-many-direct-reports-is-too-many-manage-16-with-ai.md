Your first AI management workflow, this week You don't need nine screens or your own app to start. We started with one rule. Four steps. The first one fits in an afternoon. 1. Pick one repetitive management task The one you dread on Friday is a good candidate. For us it was the weekly status check. 2. Write down how you decide it, as 3 to 5 rules If writing feels hard, let the agent interview you. Open Claude or ChatGPT and say: I want to automate this, ask me questions, and at the end write me an MD file with the rules. Adam's example for a Friday status check: "Every Friday I check whether my projects are on track. Here are the five things I look for. Help me evaluate this week's updates." 3. Let AI apply them, and fix the rules where it's wrong "Don't care about the tokens," at the start. Run everything through AI and look at the output. When it's wrong, don't correct the result by hand. "Go and fix the rules so that you can repeat it next time." Turn the rules into a script later, once they stop changing. 4. With every new model, run the same rules again Some of Adam's attempts failed because AI could not reach a company portal or the process was too complex.
