# Can your SaaS make the top-five builder leaderboard?

## Overview

Most launch posts ask you to read a thread, watch a demo, or sign up for another waitlist. I wanted something a little more fun: a tiny founder challenge where SaaS makers can compete instead. BuilderBench is a free web game for builders. It gives you eight events, one all-round score, and a leaderboard where the top five scores can show their SaaS links. The events are: Reaction Time Sequence Memory Aim Trainer Number Memory Verbal Memory Chimp Test Visual Memory Typing That mix is funny because it accidentally tests a bunch of founder traits: Can you react fast without overthinking? Can you remember the next step after ten distractions? Can you stay accurate under pressure? Can you type cleanly when your brain is moving faster than your hands? It is not trying to be a serious productivity framework. It is just a quick, competitive builder benchmark. If your SaaS makes the top five, your link gets shown there. No paid placement, no weird growth-hack promise, just a simple leaderboard challenge for people building things. Try it here: https://www.buildersbenchmark.com/ My favorite part is that it gives founders a better call to action than “please check out my launch.” Instead, it is: Can you beat the other builders?
