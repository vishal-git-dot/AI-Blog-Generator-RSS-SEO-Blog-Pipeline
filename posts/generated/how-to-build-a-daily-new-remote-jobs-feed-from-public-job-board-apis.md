# How to build a daily "new remote jobs" feed from public job-board APIs

## Overview

You want a daily list of new remote jobs for yourself, a newsletter or a small job board. Several boards publish a JSON API or an RSS feed for exactly this. Each one comes with attribution rules and polling limits, though, and the same job often shows up on two boards. Boards with open feeds I called two of these for this post and read the terms of the rest on 2026-11-11. Re-read each board's page before you ship anything. Remote OK: https://remoteok.com/api is one JSON request. Element 0 is a legal notice, then the ~100 most recent jobs (98 today). The terms: link back to the job with a followed link (no nofollow ), name Remote OK as the source, and do not use its logo. We Work Remotely: RSS per category, for example https://weworkremotely.com/categories/remote-programming-jobs.rss (25 items today). Anyone may use the feeds; attribute the links back to the board. Remotive: https://remotive.com/api/remote-jobs , published with a 24-hour delay and meant to be polled about four times a day at most. Link back, mention Remotive, and do not forward its jobs to Google Jobs, LinkedIn Jobs, Jooble or Neuvoo. Jobicy: https://jobicy.com/api/jobs?count=100 , the last 7 days, no more than once an hour. Credit Jobicy and keep apply links on its job page. Others: 4dayweek.io allows 60 requests a minute and asks for a credit; Remote First Jobs has the same "do not forward" rule as Remotive; Arbeitnow is a free API for mostly German jobs, "do not abuse". Terms differ enough to matter. At least one board with an open API, Himalayas, forbids passing its jobs on to third parties, so I left it out. Only a match across different boards is treated as a duplicate. Limits and gotchas Attribution is part of the deal. Show the source board and link to its job URL on every item. "New" means new to the feed. Some feeds are delayed by hours or a day, and some only show a recent window, so a job that drops out of the window has not necessarily closed. Respect the windows. Cache what you fetch and poll no faster than each board says. Do not republish a board's jobs somewhere its terms forbid. No workarounds. Boards behind bot protection or without a public feed are out of scope; one board answered 403 to a plain request while I was building the Actor. Move on instead of fighting it.
