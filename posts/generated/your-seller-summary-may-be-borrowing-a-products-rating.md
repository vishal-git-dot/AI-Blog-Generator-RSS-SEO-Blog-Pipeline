# Your Seller Summary May Be Borrowing a Product's Rating

Your agent can read the right rating and still rate the wrong thing. You wire a seller check into your weekend agent project. The summary sounds reassuring, but its evidence includes the rating of something the shop sells. Excellent kettle. Unanswered question about the shop. Lab note 093: can we keep product reputation out of a seller summary before the model sees it? The records below are synthetic, and public profile data supports a check rather than completing a vetting decision. Why does seller mode mix reputation? Seller mode emits catalog products by default, so name and rating can describe different entities in the same dataset. The dataset holds four record types, distinguished by recordType .
