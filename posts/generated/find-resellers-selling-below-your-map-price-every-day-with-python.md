# Find resellers selling below your MAP price every day with Python

## Overview

If you sell a product through other shops, you need to know which of them list it below your minimum advertised price (MAP), and you need to know it the day it happens, not at the end of the month. This is part 3 of a small series. In part 1 I compared one product's price across stores, and in part 2 I added a change mode that returns only offers whose price or stock moved. This post adds the MAP check I built on top of it this week: give it your MAP and it flags every offer below it, with the gap in money and in percent. Real output from today (2026-11-14) is below, plus the limits. Why the usual approach hurts Most small brands check MAP like this: Someone opens 20 to 200 reseller pages in a browser, once a week if there is time. They copy prices into a spreadsheet, some in USD, some in EUR or GBP. They compare each one with the MAP column by hand. A reseller drops the price on Monday, and the brand finds out on Friday, after other resellers have already matched it. A script helps, but then you own the scraper, the currency conversion, the per-product MAP table and the "what changed since yesterday" logic. That last part is the one that breaks. What the tool does I built the Cheapest Price Finder on Apify. You give it product page URLs from any stores. It reads the real price from each page's structured data (JSON-LD offers, Shopify product data, meta price tags), converts everything to one currency with daily ECB rates and sorts the offers from cheapest to most expensive. The MAP check adds three inputs: mapPrice : your MAP, in the convertTo currency. mapPrices (optional): a different MAP per product URL, as {url: price} . It overrides mapPrice for that URL. onlyChanged + monitorId (from part 2): return only new or changed offers, so a daily run is quiet unless something moved. Every row then gets mapPrice , belowMap (true/false), mapGap (how far below MAP, in money; negative means above MAP) and mapGapPercent . RUN_SUMMARY.mapCheck lists belowMapCount and belowMapUrls , so one key tells you whether today needs attention. Keep yesterday's rows yourself there, or use the Apify actor for the built-in change feed. Honest limits Big marketplaces often block automated requests. Amazon, Walmart and Best Buy frequently answer with a bot-check page. The tool does not bypass that; such pages land in RUN_SUMMARY.failed and are not charged. Brand stores and independent shops (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce) work best. It reads the listed price only : no coupons, cart discounts or shipping. A reseller who hides the real price in the cart will not show up. You choose the URLs. It does not find your resellers for you or match products; gtin , sku and brand in each row help you check. Pages that build the price only with JavaScript and publish no product data return "no price found". It does not contact anyone. It tells you who is below MAP; what you do next is up to you.
