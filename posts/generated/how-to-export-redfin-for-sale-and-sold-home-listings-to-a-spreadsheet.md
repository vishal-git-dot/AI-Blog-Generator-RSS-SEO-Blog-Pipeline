Redfin shows all of that on its map, but copying it by hand is slow, and one search on the site stops at 350 homes. The Redfin Home Listings Scraper: For Sale and Sold Homes , published by Hay Equipos on the Apify Store, takes ZIP codes, cities or Redfin search URLs and returns one clean row per home. It reads the same public search data the Redfin website loads in your browser. No login, no account. This is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Redfin.
