# Designing Power-Ups Around a Theme: The Hydro Robot Runner

## Overview

Power-ups are easy to add and hard to make meaningful. A speed boost, a shield, a magnet: every runner has them. In AI Rush, a free 3D endless runner you play in the browser, I tried to let the theme decide what the power-ups are. This post is about that process. The premise You play Hydro AI, a robot running through a smart city. Obstacles are lasers, drones, security bots and rolling power cells. The robot runs on water and electricity, so the power-ups come from those two resources. When the theme and mechanics share a vocabulary, players learn faster. Nobody needs a tutorial to guess what "Hydro Shield" does. Two resources, four power-ups You collect water and electricity as you run. Those feed four effects: Hydro Shield protects you from a hit. Lightning Dash gets you through danger quickly. Water Jet clears the way ahead. Battery Overdrive boosts your run. I like this structure for a reason: collecting two simple things creates choice. A runner that offers one pickup type gives you no decisions. A runner with a dozen overwhelms. Two resources and four outcomes is a small enough space to understand and big enough to feel clever. Obstacles that teach Each obstacle should demand a slightly different response, otherwise they are just reskins of the same hazard. Lasers read as "go around or through the gap." Drones move, so they demand timing. Security bots occupy space, so they push you to change lane. Rolling power cells come at you, so they reward a quick reaction. If every obstacle asks the same question, the game gets dull quickly. Unlocks as a long-term goal There are 4 unlockable robots. Unlocks matter in an endless runner because there is no final level. They give a reason to keep going even when a personal best is out of reach. Why "3D" in the browser Players searching for 3D endless runner games or 3D running games are often disappointed by pages that need a plugin or a download. AI Rush runs in the browser with no install. That constraint pushed me to keep scenes light and the obstacle set readable at speed on a phone. Takeaways Let the theme name the mechanics. Keep the resource space small. Make each hazard ask a different question. Give endless games something to unlock. Try it AI Rush is free to play in the browser , with no sign-up.
