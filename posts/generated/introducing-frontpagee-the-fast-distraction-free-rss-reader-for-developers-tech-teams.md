# Introducing Frontpagee: The Fast, Distraction-Free RSS Reader for Developers & Tech Teams

## Overview

Today, we are thrilled to officially introduce Frontpagee — a modern, keyboard-driven RSS reader and feed aggregator engineered for developers, engineering teams, and curious readers who want to stay informed without algorithmic noise. The Problem: The Decline of the Open Web Reader Over the past decade, two major shifts ruined how technical professionals consume information: The Algorithmic Trap: Social platforms and feed algorithms are optimized for outrage and engagement bait rather than depth, technical signal, and focus. Enterprise Bloat: Legacy RSS readers like Feedly abandoned developers and individual readers, pivoting into $12/month enterprise cybersecurity suites with paywalled keyboard shortcuts and cluttered interfaces. We built Frontpagee to restore the simplicity, speed, and joy of reading the independent web. ⚡ Built for Developers & Power Users Frontpagee delivers a distraction-free, chronological stream of content from your favorite engineering blogs, GitHub release feeds, substacks, and tech publications: Pure Chronological Signal: No opaque recommendation algorithms or sponsored clutter. You only see updates from the sources you choose to follow. Keyboard-First Workflow: Navigate through articles with Vim-inspired shortcuts ( J for next, K for previous, O to open original source) designed for high-efficiency reading. Zero-Lock-in 1-Click OPML Migration: Seamlessly export or import your existing feed directory from Feedly, Inoreader, or any standard XML feed file in seconds. Curated Topic Collections: Pre-bundled directories for React, AI Research, DevOps, Architecture, and Open Source to jumpstart your daily reading stack. Offline-Ready Progressive Web App (PWA): Instant local caching and lightning-fast page transitions across desktop and mobile. 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack Frontpagee is built with a lightweight, ultra-responsive modern stack: Frontend: React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vite Real-time Sync & Storage: Firebase (Firestore, Auth, Cloud Functions) AI Summarization: Google Gemini API for fast, contextual TL;DRs Reader Rendering: Custom DOM sanitize pipeline optimized for clean readability and dark mode fidelity. 🚀 Try Frontpagee Today Frontpagee is completely free to use: 👉 Launch App: frontpagee.in We are actively iterating on our product roadmap.
