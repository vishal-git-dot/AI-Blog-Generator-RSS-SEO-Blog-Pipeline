Keep Your Cigars Fresh A well-kept cigar tastes noticeably better than one that has dried out. Backwoods come in sealed packaging that protects them until the first open, but after that, a little care goes a long way. • Keep cigars in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat. • Reseal the pack tightly after each use, or move cigars to an airtight container. • Avoid leaving them in a hot car or near a window, where temperature swings can dry the leaf. • If you store them for weeks at a time, consider a small humidity pouch to maintain moisture.
