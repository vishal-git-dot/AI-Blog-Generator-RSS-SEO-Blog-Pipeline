# 4 Ways Small Sites Actually Get Told a Keyword Ranking Dropped

## Overview

Most ranking drops get noticed weeks late, because the only way most people check is opening a dashboard they forgot existed. Here are 4 real approaches that push the data to you instead. Google Search Console's free Performance report. No alerts and no email, but position data for every query your site already ranks for, updated daily, at zero cost. Good for a manual spot check, not for catching a drop the day it happens. Threshold alerts inside a full SEO suite. Tools like AccuRanker and SEOClarity's Rank Intelligence fire an email or SMS once a keyword moves past a rule you set, such as dropping out of the top 10. They work well, but you are paying for backlink analysis and site audits you may never open, just to get the alert feature. RankWatch-style threshold email on a lighter plan. A notification when a first-page keyword falls to page two, without the full agency dashboard price tag, for teams that mainly want the drop flagged. A fixed weekly email with no login at all. SEO Monitor sends a Monday ranking table for 20 tracked keywords with week over week movement, at a flat price per site, built around the inbox instead of a dashboard. A full breakdown of what each approach actually costs and delivers is at gmcsuspension.com/seo/website-ranking-tracker-email-alerts.html . The right pick depends on whether you want one more feature inside a suite you already pay for, or a single email that is the whole product.
