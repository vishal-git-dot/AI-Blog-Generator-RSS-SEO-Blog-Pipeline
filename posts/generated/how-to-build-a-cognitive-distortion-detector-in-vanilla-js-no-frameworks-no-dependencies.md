# How to Build a Cognitive Distortion Detector in Vanilla JS (No Frameworks, No Dependencies)

## Overview

You don't need React, Vue, or any NLP library to detect cognitive distortions in text. Here's a complete, working detector in under 150 lines of vanilla JS. What Are Cognitive Distortions? Cognitive distortions are biased thinking patterns that fuel anxiety and depression. Aaron Beck identified 10 common ones in CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy). A detector scans text for these patterns and flags them — the first step in helping someone reframe their thinking.
