# Mastering Video Hooks with Logic Gap

## Overview

Stop losing viewers in the first 30 seconds. If your retention graph drops off a cliff right after your intro, your script is missing one critical element: the Logic Gap . The Logic Gap is a copywriting framework designed to trigger immediate, irrepressible curiosity. The formula is simple: present two true facts that seem to completely contradict each other. Human brains crave order. When presented with a logical paradox, cognitive dissonance kicks in, and the brain demands a resolution. The only way your viewer can resolve the tension? Keeping their eyes glued to your video. Most tech creators pitch videos like this: "Today, we're reviewing this new $300 Android phone to see if it's any good." Boring. Skip. Now, rewrite it using a Logic Gap: "This $300 phone takes sharper night photos than a $1,200 iPhone 15 Pro... yet top tech reviewers are begging you not to buy it." See the shift? Fact A: It outperforms a luxury flagship. Fact B: Experts say avoid it. The Gap: Why? Here is another one for software and gaming channels: "Linux is completely free, uses half the RAM of Windows, and has zero tracking. So why do 95% of PC gamers refuse to touch it?" To build a Logic Gap in your next tech script, follow three steps: Identify the flex: What makes the tech fast, cheap, or hyped? Drop the catch: What is the bizarre downside, counter-intuitive truth, or strange failure? Withhold the bridge: Frame the contradiction immediately, but don't resolve it until deeper in the video.
