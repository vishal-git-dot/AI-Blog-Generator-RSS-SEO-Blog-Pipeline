# Building a Modern Browser Toolkit for Guitarists: Web Audio Tuner, Chord Transposition & AI Stem Separation

## Overview

Building a modern web-based toolkit for musicians requires combining fast client-side audio processing, clean typography for chord sheets, and AI-powered stem separation. When we designed Akordha (آکوردها) , our goal was to move beyond static lyric-and-chord pages and give guitarists an all-in-one browser studio. 1. Real-Time Browser Guitar Tuner & Metronome Using the Web Audio API ( AudioContext , AnalyserNode , and autocorrelation pitch detection), musicians can tune their instruments directly in the browser without installing heavy native apps. Try the live Online Guitar Tuner for standard 6-string pitch detection. Pair practice sessions with the Online Metronome for steady rhythm control. 2. Dynamic Chord Transposition & Interactive Fingerings Traditional chord archives often break formatting on mobile screens or lack instant key transposition. In our Persian Guitar Chords Archive , every song supports: Real-time semitone transposition and capo suggestions. Visual fretboard fingering diagrams and synthesized chord audio previews. Smart auto-scrolling synchronized for hands-free playing. 3. Multi-Track Backing Tracks & AI Vocal Separation For soloists and learners, practicing over isolated stems makes a huge difference. Alongside our curated Guitar Backing Tracks library, Akordha AI Studio uses deep-learning stem separation to isolate vocals, guitar, drums, and bass from any audio track. If you are interested in music tech, Web Audio API DSP, or Persian guitar resources, check out akordha.ir .
