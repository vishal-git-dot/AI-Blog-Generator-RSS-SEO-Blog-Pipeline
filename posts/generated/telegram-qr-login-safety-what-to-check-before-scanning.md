# Telegram QR login safety: what to check before scanning

## Overview

When people search for Telegram help, the fastest answer is not always the safest one. A safer approach is to separate the problem into source, session, privacy, and recovery checks. This article focuses on QR login source checks, shared browsers, and ending web sessions. 1. Start with the account state Before changing settings, confirm whether the account is already active on another device. Telegram often sends important login or security prompts to existing sessions first. If an old phone, desktop app, or web session is still active, check it before repeatedly requesting new codes or reinstalling clients. 2. Check the source before the setting Many beginner problems begin with an unclear source: a search result, a forwarded link, a group file, or a page that claims to be a special Chinese version. Avoid treating language support, login help, and installation source as the same thing. Use a trusted client or web entry first, then adjust language and privacy settings. 3. Review sessions and recovery options Active sessions are the fastest way to discover whether the account is still tied to an old browser or desktop. End sessions you do not recognize, then review two-step verification and recovery email. This is especially important before changing phones or using Telegram Web on a shared computer. 4. Keep privacy settings simple For beginners, the most useful privacy checks are phone number visibility, who can find you by number, who can invite you to groups, profile photo visibility, and forwarded-message attribution. You do not need to hide everything, but you should know which settings expose identity to strangers. 5. Use a step-by-step reference For Chinese-speaking users who want a complete walkthrough, this reference is useful: Telegram Web login safety guide . It is better to follow one clear checklist than to jump between random answers, helper bots, and unofficial download pages. Final note The goal is not to make Telegram complicated. The goal is to keep the account recoverable, the device list clean, and the privacy settings understandable. If a page asks for a login code, recovery email, or two-step verification password outside the normal Telegram flow, stop and verify the source before continuing.
