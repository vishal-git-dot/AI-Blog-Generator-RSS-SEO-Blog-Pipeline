In this article, I will try to share a few points that I’ve understood over the 3 years of working on code. Well, let’s get started! 🏎️ 🔎 Understanding the target audience of the product The most basic and simplest thing possible. Why does anyone need your product at all? Who should use it in their work? And many similar questions. Understand that just because you found something interesting to do rather than playing games or watching shows doesn’t mean it’s as important to others as it is to you. This only means that you’ve created a very slim chance that someone will find your project useful. Today, with AI, your products are no longer needed by anyone, because there are so many of them that people are already sick of yet another open‑source project that will simply stop being supported until the author runs out of money or finds a new project to develop. Understanding this, you will see that people don’t register in the app or buy a subscription from you not because they’re mean, but simply because your project doesn’t matter. So, for the project to matter at all, you need to create a unique value proposition. The reason why people will choose you and not anyone else.
