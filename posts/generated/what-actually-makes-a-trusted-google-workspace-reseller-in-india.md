# What Actually Makes a "Trusted" Google Workspace Reseller in India?

## Overview

"Trusted" gets used a lot in Google Workspace reseller comparisons without much behind it, usually just meaning "this company seems legitimate." Not a very useful bar, since every authorized reseller sells the identical product, same Gmail, Drive, Docs, and admin console, regardless of who sells it. If the product doesn't change, trust has to mean something more specific: verified partner status, an operating history you can actually check, and honesty about a company's current structure, not what an older article once said. I checked seven established names in the Indian market against Google's own Partner Directory and independent coverage where possible, instead of taking each company's marketing at face value. The seven, and what each is actually trading on Leads Monky — Google Cloud Partner offering Workspace plus a separate line in B2B lead gen and marketing. Trust case here is transparency, not history: pricing is published directly, no sales call required. Business Starter: $3/user/month (<300 users), $2.50/user/month (300+) No minimum user count required Shivaami (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai) — close to a decade built specifically around security and compliance, a recurring pick for healthcare and finance. Trust through demonstrated expertise where getting it wrong actually matters. Searce (Pune) — leans on scale and recognition, named Google Cloud Partner of the Year multiple times, with delivery teams across India, the US, the UK, and Asia Pacific. MediaAgility — this is the one that actually reframes the whole exercise. Still described as independent in a striking number of currently published comparison articles. It isn't. Acquired by Persistent Systems, a publicly listed global tech services company, back in 2022, confirmed directly on Google Cloud's own Partner Directory. If an article can't get a company's current ownership right, its other claims deserve real skepticism too. Wroffy Technologies (Gurugram) — trust through longevity, one of the oldest Premier Workspace partners in India. Amyntas Media Works (Gurugram) — trust through breadth, over a decade across healthcare, education, manufacturing, retail, and IT, with stated 24x7 post-deployment support. ResellerClub — institutional backing, genuine Indian roots now under the larger Newfold Digital group, India-specific pricing at ₹249/user/month. The actual takeaway None of these seven trust profiles is more valid than the others. What matters is unpacking "trusted" into something checkable: Confirm Google Cloud Partner tier through Google's own Partner Directory, not a badge on the site Ask specifically what migration and support include, not a vague promise Check whether ownership has changed recently (see: MediaAgility) rather than trusting an older article Important Points "Trusted" means nothing on its own since the product is identical across every reseller MediaAgility was quietly acquired by Persistent Systems in 2022; most current lists still miss this The real trust signal is whether a company's claims still hold up when you go check them yourself, not the claims themselves Has anyone here actually verified a reseller's partner tier directly through Google's directory before signing up, or does everyone just take the website badge at face value?
