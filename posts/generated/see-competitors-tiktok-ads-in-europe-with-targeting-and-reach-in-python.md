# See competitors' TikTok ads in Europe, with targeting and reach, in Python

## Overview

Under the EU Digital Services Act, TikTok publishes the ads shown in the EU/EEA, Switzerland, the UK and Turkey in a public library: who ran it, who paid for it, when it ran, how many people saw it, and the targeting behind it, down to reach by country, age and gender. For a marketer that is a view into a competitor's TikTok strategy. The library is built for looking up one ad at a time, though, not for a table of every ad a brand ran this month or a morning list of what is new. This post pulls those ads into Python, builds a competitor sheet with targeting and reach, and sets up a daily "new ads only" watch. Disclosure: I'm Aurelix, and I built the TikTok Ads Library API .
