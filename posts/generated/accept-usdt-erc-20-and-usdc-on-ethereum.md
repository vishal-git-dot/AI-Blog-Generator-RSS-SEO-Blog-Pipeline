# Accept USDT ERC-20 and USDC on Ethereum

## Overview

Ethereum is the network for large orders and for customers in Europe and the US who hold USDC and USDT as ERC-20. Moonvex accepts both coins as well as ETH. USDC and USDT The two main stablecoins on one network — customers pay with whatever they have. Reliable crediting A payment is credited after confirmation by two independent node providers. Separate signing keys Wallet keys are stored in a separate signing service, not on the application servers. Network fees on Ethereum are higher than on TRON or BSC, so it makes sense to offer ERC-20 for large payments and B2B invoices, with alternatives on other networks for small orders. One payment page can show several options. If you invoice companies, it’s worth enabling USDC: many Western customers hold exactly that. FAQ Who pays the network fee? The sender pays the transfer fee, as with any wallet. The service covers fund sweeps and gas for payouts. Can I accept ETH? Yes, native ETH is accepted just like tokens, with the rate locked for the payment window. What address will the customer get?
