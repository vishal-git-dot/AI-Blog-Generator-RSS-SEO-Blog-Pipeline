A stored conversation continued with previous_response_id counts that history as input. Cross 200K and you pay long-context rates in full, not only for the tokens past 200K . Default is max, and none is a 400 Wonder, Spark, and Ember generate internal reasoning that stays out of the response body, but those tokens count toward max_tokens / max_output_tokens and billed output. If most of the budget goes to reasoning, the visible answer can truncate. The effort ladder is minimal , low , medium , high , xhigh , and max . "none" is not supported and returns HTTP 400 . When you omit the parameter, Wonder and Ember default to max ; Spark defaults to high . Meta describes max as going beyond xhigh : more reasoning tokens, same per-token price. That is the other invoice surprise. A "cheap subagent" call with no effort set is a max call. Set the knob on every request, pick the lowest level that works for that subtask, and set the output cap high enough for reasoning plus visible text. When to use Ember vs Spark vs Wonder Ember when a larger model plans and sub-agents run many bounded tasks: classification, extraction, tool calls, terminal subtasks, or the same job over a pile of files or tickets. Keep prompts under 200K input for the cheap band. If a session grows past that line, summarize or truncate before the flip, or move the long-horizon pass to Spark or Wonder. Spark ( muse-spark-1.3 is the recommended id) for the main agent loop, or harder work at a lower cost than Wonder. Pricing is flat at any context length.
