# Google's November Pixel Drop Brings Gemini Intelligence to Pixel 10 and Launches Pixel Tag

## Overview

The November Pixel Drop is here, and it does what Pixel Drops do best: make older phones feel new again. Gemini Intelligence lands on Pixel 10, Call Screen gets smarter on Pixel 11, and Google is launching a brand-new gadget — Pixel Tag — on sale from November 11. Gemini Intelligence on Pixel 10 Gemini Intelligence is the headline. It gives Pixel 10 a smarter Gemini — more natural and expressive in conversation, with proactive suggestions and routines that follow your daily habits. One new piece is Rambler, which taps into your messages, emails and calendar to surface suggestions tied to the day ahead. Gemini Live can also handle multi-step tasks for you — Google's example is placing your morning coffee order. It's the phone doing the errands, not just answering questions. Call Screen gets an upgrade Pixel 11 owners get the other big phone change: Call Screen sounds more natural now, and it can check who is calling and why before you pick up. For the rest of the line, the Drop still brings plenty — the whole point of a Pixel Drop is that the phone you own gets better, not just the one Google wants to sell you. Pixel Tag, priced The surprise is hardware. Pixel Tag is Google's new tracker — a slim, keychain-ready tag for finding everyday items. It's available from November 11 at $29.99 for one, or $99.99 for a four-pack. Read the full announcement in Google's November Pixel Drop post . Pixel 10 owners get a smarter phone today; Pixel 11 owners get a better gatekeeper for their calls; and anyone prone to losing their keys can spend thirty bucks on insurance. Gemini has been busy lately — and Google's TPU chips are the hardware behind a lot of it. The Drop itself is free, as ever: it lands as a software update, and the only thing with a price tag is the Tag. Originally published on Rohan Tech Official .
