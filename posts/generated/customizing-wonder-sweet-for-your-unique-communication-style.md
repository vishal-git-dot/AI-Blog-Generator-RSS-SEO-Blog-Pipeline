# Customizing Wonder Sweet for Your Unique Communication Style

## Overview

One size does not fit all when it comes to communication. Wonder Sweet's strength is its ability to adapt to your unique voice and style. This tutorial covers the customization options available. Personalizing the AI The sweet interactions engine learns from your messages. Over time, it picks up on your vocabulary, sentence structure, and typical expressions. The more you use it, the more it sounds like you. Adjusting Suggestion Frequency Some users want suggestions on every message. Others prefer occasional help. Wonder Sweet lets you adjust how frequently the AI offers suggestions. Tone Preferences Set your default tone preferences for each mode. Do you prefer your professional mode to be warm or formal? Your romantic mode to be passionate or subtle? The sweet interactions feature respects these preferences. Creating Custom Personas The Expert Role Library allows you to create custom communication personas. Combine elements from different default personas to create something uniquely yours. Privacy Controls Customize what data the AI uses for learning. You can opt to use only the current conversation context without saving patterns. The sweet interactions engine becomes more personalized the more you use it. Here is a timeline of what to expect: Week 1: Generic but helpful suggestions Week 2: Increasingly relevant recommendations Week 3: Suggestions that match your voice closely Week 4: AI that anticipates your needs Customization is key to getting the most out of Wonder Sweet. Invest time in setting up your preferences, and the sweet interactions feature will reward you with increasingly accurate and personalized suggestions.
