# How to Trim Audio for a Maple Lagoon Video — Free, in Your Browser

## Overview

A Maple Lagoon video is the kind of clip that lives or dies on its mood — still water, warm autumn color, and a slow drift. The music has to match, which usually means trimming a longer track down to the exact stretch that fits your footage. You can do it for free in your browser with AudioCutter — no install, no account, and your file never leaves your computer. Step 1: Open AudioCutter and load your track Go to AudioCutter and choose your audio file. MP3, WAV, M4A, and most other common formats work. The waveform appears in a few seconds, and nothing is uploaded — the file stays on your device. Step 2: Drag the handles to keep the part you want Listen once through, then drag the start and end handles to the section that fits your lagoon footage. For a slow, calm scene, keep a few seconds of breathing room at the start rather than cutting straight into the melody. Step 3: Add a short fade so the ending doesn't cut off Use the fade-out handle on the last second or two. A gentle fade keeps the music from stopping abruptly when the video ends, which matters a lot in a quiet nature clip. Step 4: Export as MP3 and drop it into your video Export the trimmed file, download the MP3, and add it to your video editor as the soundtrack. Check the volume against your footage once — lagoon scenes usually sit better with the music a little lower than you first think. A trimmed track takes a minute and makes the whole video feel deliberate instead of accidental. Keep the original file, so you can cut a different length later if the edit changes.
