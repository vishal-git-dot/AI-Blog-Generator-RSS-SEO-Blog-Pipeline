# Certified Data Analytics: Building Practical Skills for Modern Analytics

## Overview

Data analytics is no longer limited to specialist teams. Businesses increasingly rely on structured information to understand performance, identify patterns, and support everyday decisions. GSDC’s Data Analytics Certification is designed to develop practical knowledge across analysis, statistics, visualization, data tools, artificial intelligence, and business intelligence. A Curriculum Designed Around Real Analytics Work The program follows the journey from collecting information to presenting insights. Learners explore: • Data collection, cleaning, transformation, and standardization • Exploratory data analysis and statistical techniques • Visualization, dashboards, and data storytelling • SQL, Excel, Python, Pandas, and NumPy • Databases, ETL, machine learning, and AI • Big data, cloud analytics, and business intelligence This structure gives learners exposure to both foundational concepts and modern analytics practices. The syllabus also includes synthetic data, automated sentiment analysis, AI-assisted analysis, and responsible AI use. Practical Learning, Not Just Theory GSDC includes 16+ hours of learning, two practice exams, a capstone project, and an AI interview practice platform. Learners also receive self-paced expert-led videos, an e-learning library, an ebook, SME Connect sessions, LinkedIn enhancement, resume support, and job support. These resources make the learning experience useful for professionals who want to connect analytical concepts with practical projects and workplace communication. Professionals Who May Benefit The certification is relevant to data analysts, business analysts, data scientists, business intelligence professionals, marketing and financial analysts, project managers, operations managers, product managers, consultants, risk and compliance analysts, and IT professionals transitioning to data roles. Previous data analytics knowledge or work experience is recommended, but GSDC states that it is not mandatory. Credential and Assessment The data analytics professional certification exam contains 40 multiple-choice questions. Candidates have 90 minutes and need a 65% score to pass. The examination is in English, is not open book, and the credential is valid for five years. A complimentary retake is provided. For professionals seeking a certified data analytics credential, this assessment provides a formal way to validate the knowledge covered by the program and apply it in business settings.
