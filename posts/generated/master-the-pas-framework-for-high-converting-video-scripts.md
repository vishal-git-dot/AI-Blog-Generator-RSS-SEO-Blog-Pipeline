# Master the PAS Framework for High-Converting Video Scripts

## Overview

Your viewers have the attention span of a goldfish on caffeine. If your tech video opens with a 15-second animated intro or a dry "Hey guys, welcome back," they’re already clicking away. Enter the PAS Framework : Problem, Agitate, Solution. It’s the highest-converting copywriting structure in advertising, and it works like pure magic for tech retention. Here is how to break it down in your next script: 1. Problem Call out a specific, highly relatable tech pain point within the first five seconds. Skip the fluff. Example: "Your brand-new MacBook Pro is thermal throttling during simple 4K edits." 2. Agitate Twist the knife. Highlight the frustration, wasted money, or ruined workflow so the viewer feels the annoyance all over again. You want them screaming, “Yes! That happens to me!” Example: "You just dropped two grand on Apple silicon, but your render times are crawling, your fans sound like a jet engine, and your deadline is in two hours." 3. Solution Deliver the payoff. Position your video, setting tweak, or product as the hero that fixes the pain. Example: "Here’s a hidden, free macOS setting that stops throttling cold and cuts export times in half." Why does this work so well for tech creators? Tech audiences are inherently searching for efficiency. They don't care about a spec sheet until they know why those specs matter to their daily life. Stop listing RAM benchmarks in your hooks. Lead with friction, make it burn, and then provide the relief. Master PAS, and your average view duration will skyrocket. Automate your video scripts for free at HookForge AI .
