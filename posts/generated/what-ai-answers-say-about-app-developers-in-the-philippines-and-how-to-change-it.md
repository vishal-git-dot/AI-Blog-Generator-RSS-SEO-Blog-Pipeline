# What AI answers say about app developers in the Philippines, and how to change it

## Overview

Here is a working checklist on GEO and AI search for app developers. It reflects what we see when companies expand into the Philippines in 2026. Build a prompt set before you change anything Write 25 to 40 prompts a buyer in the Philippines might ask: category questions, comparisons, 'best tool for' questions and pricing questions. Run them in the main assistants every month and record three things: is the brand mentioned, is it recommended, and is your page cited as a source. This baseline turns GEO from opinion into a measurable project. Technical hygiene Check robots.txt and your CDN settings so AI crawlers you want are not blocked by accident. Publish an up-to-date sitemap, use descriptive titles, and add structured data only where the content is visible. Keep language versions linked with hreflang so the right market version is quoted. A 90-day pilot Month one: facts page, prompt baseline, technical fixes. Month two: five to eight answer pages and directory clean-up. Month three: re-run the prompt set, compare, and decide which topics to expand. A pilot keeps the budget small and the learning concrete. Off-site signals matter Assistants also read directories, review sites, documentation hubs and community answers. Make sure your company description is consistent everywhere it appears. Inconsistent names, old pricing or outdated product lists on third-party pages are a common reason an assistant describes a company incorrectly. What AI assistants need from your site Generative search tools build answers from pages they can read and trust. For app developers, that means a clear statement of what you do, who you serve, where you operate and how pricing works, written in plain sentences that can be quoted. If those facts only appear in images, PDFs or scattered blog posts, assistants guess, and guesses rarely favour you. How to report progress Report monthly on share of mention across the prompt set, number of prompts where you are recommended, number of cited URLs from your domain, and leads that mention AI search as the source. Tag those leads in your CRM so the work can be tied to revenue rather than screenshots. Pages that earn citations Assistants cite pages that answer one question well. Create focused pages for pricing logic, integrations, comparisons, implementation steps and FAQs. Put the direct answer in the first two sentences, then supporting detail, dates and sources.
