# Medical Device Complaint Handling and MDR Reporting: A Developer's Guide to 21 CFR 803 and ISO 13485 8.2.2

## Overview

Picture a support ticket on a Friday afternoon: "Unit shut off halfway through a treatment. Patient fine, restarted it." The agent tags it hardware , the queue sorts it under low priority , and nobody outside support reads it. Five weeks later a quality engineer finds three more tickets like it, decides the shutdowns are a reportable malfunction, and checks the date on the first one. The 30-day deadline passed a week ago. Medical device complaint handling is the process that stops that week from happening. Under FDA's Quality Management System Regulation (QMSR), a manufacturer must receive, review and evaluate every complaint under ISO 13485:2016 clause 8.2.2, keep the records 21 CFR 820.35 lists, and decide for each one whether it is a Medical Device Report (MDR) under 21 CFR Part 803. FDA's wider expectations for device makers sit in FDA's guidelines for medical device manufacturers . FAQ What counts as a complaint for a medical device? A complaint is any written, electronic or oral communication that alleges a deficiency in a device's identity, quality, durability, reliability, usability, safety or performance after release. A support ticket, a sales email or a phone call can each be a complaint. What are the MDR reporting deadlines? Under 21 CFR 803.50, a manufacturer must report a death, serious injury or reportable malfunction within 30 calendar days of becoming aware of it. Under 803.53, a 5-day report is due within 5 work days when the event needs remedial action to prevent an unreasonable risk of substantial harm, or when FDA requests one in writing. When does a manufacturer become aware of an MDR event? Part 803 treats a manufacturer as aware when any employee acquires information that reasonably suggests a reportable event occurred. The clock does not wait for the quality team to review the complaint. What must a complaint record contain under QMSR? For complaints that are investigated or reportable, 21 CFR 820.35(a) requires the device name, the date received, any UDI or UPC, the complainant's name, address and phone number, the nature and details of the complaint, any correction or corrective action, and any reply to the complainant. Does complaint handling software need validation? Yes. Software that a manufacturer uses for complaint handling and MDR decisions is quality system software, and ISO 13485 clause 4.1.6 requires validation for its intended use.
