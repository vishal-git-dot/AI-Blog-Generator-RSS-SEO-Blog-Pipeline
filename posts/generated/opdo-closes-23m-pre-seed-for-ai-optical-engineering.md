# Opdo closes $2.3M pre-seed for AI optical engineering

## Overview

Opdo, which describes its business as optical engineering built natively around artificial intelligence, has secured $2.3 million in pre-seed financing, Tech. eu reports . The startup operates from Los Angeles and Berlin. Angular Ventures led the round, with Inflection and Tiny.vc taking part. What Opdo builds The company is developing a self-improving system that joins AI-driven design and simulation with data from real manufacturing. Its stated goal is to automate optical engineering from the first design through to finished components. The firm says it has already cut development and production timelines for end-to-end builds from months down to weeks. Over the past year and more, Opdo has partnered with clients on intricate optical systems, combining AI agents with expert engineers. Its technology runs inside established optical design software, including Zemax and CODE V, so engineers keep their current tools. Customers can also source prototypes and finished components through the company. Projects so far cover laser and imaging systems, micro-optics and nanostructured surfaces. The feedback loop The core idea is that data from manufactured and tested components flows back into the AI models. Each later design benefits from what earlier parts showed in production, which Opdo says moves the process step by step toward production-ready systems built from initial requirements. The company points to optics demand in quantum computing, aerospace, AR/VR, defence and advanced sensing, and to a shortage of specialist engineers as the bottleneck. Who is behind it Opdo was founded by Stephan Kuehr (CEO) and Yousef Arzhangnia (CTO). Kuehr previously co-founded 3YOURMIND, a software company that automates distributed manufacturing. The new money is earmarked for expanding the engineering team, developing the AI tooling and taking on more customer projects. Companies with an optical design project can submit a use case on the Opdo website. What is and is not confirmed The months-to-weeks figure comes from the company and its founders; the source gives no customer names, benchmarks or methodology for it. This is an early-stage raise, and the product is currently delivered with Opdo's own engineers involved, not as a self-serve tool.
