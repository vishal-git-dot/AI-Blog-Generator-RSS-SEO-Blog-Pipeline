# I Needed a Square Profile Photo — I Compared 3 Free Image Croppers

## Overview

I was updating my profile photo everywhere — GitHub, LinkedIn, a forum I post on — and every single one of them wanted a square image. The photo I liked was a landscape shot from a trip, with me off to one side and a lot of empty sky on the other. I didn't want to install anything for a five-minute job. I just wanted to crop it square, keep my face in the frame, and download a clean result. So I tried three free options and compared them on that one photo. 1. My phone's built-in photo editor I tried my phone's Photos app first, since it was already there. It let me crop to a square, and I could drag the frame around until my face was centred. It worked, but the result stayed on my phone, and I still had to send it to my laptop to use it anywhere else. The square presets were also limited — it was either a free crop or a fixed square, with no way to type in an exact size. For a quick social avatar it was fine, but it felt like a workaround rather than a tool for the job. 2. An online image cropper Next I tried a popular online cropper I found through search. It opened in the browser, I picked my photo, and I could drag a square crop box over it and download the result. The catch was the upload: my photo went to their server before I could crop it. It was just a holiday photo, so it didn't matter much, but I noticed the page was also full of pop-ups and a timer counting down before the download link appeared. It got the job done, but the whole thing felt slower than it needed to be. 3. zippic Then I tried https://zippic.app/image-cropper for the same photo. It runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded — the file stays on your own device. You open the page, drop the photo in, drag the crop box to a square, and download the result. You can pick a preset ratio like 1:1 or set your own width and height, and it keeps the original quality. I centred the square on my face, downloaded the PNG, and checked it at full size. It was sharp, the colours were unchanged, and the file was ready to upload everywhere. No account, no pop-ups, no waiting. What I compared Square crop? Upload? Signup? I cropped a square copy with https://zippic.app/image-cropper , checked it at full size, and used it as my profile photo on all three sites. It took about two minutes. The phone editor is fine if you're already on your phone and just need something quick. If I need a square crop at an exact size — or I don't want to upload the photo anywhere — I open zippic now.
