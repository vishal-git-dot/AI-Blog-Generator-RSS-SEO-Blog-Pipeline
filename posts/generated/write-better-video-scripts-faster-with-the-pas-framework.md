# Write Better Video Scripts Faster with the PAS Framework

## Overview

Retention dropping off a cliff in the first 30 seconds? You’re likely losing viewers because your opening hook reads like a spec sheet instead of a story. If you want to keep tech enthusiasts glued to their screens, you need a framework designed for human psychology. Meet PAS : Problem, Agitate, Solve. It’s a dead-simple, three-step structure that turns dry tech topics into high-retention videos. 1. Problem Start by identifying a hyper-specific pain point your viewer is actively dealing with. Skip the drawn-out channel intros and hit them right where it hurts. Tech Example: "Your brand-new M3 MacBook is thermal throttling, and your 4K video render just crashed—again." 2. Agitate Twist the knife. Remind them why this problem sucks. Evoke the frustration, the lost time, or the money drained from their wallet. Make them feel the urgency to fix it. Tech Example: "You’re sitting there watching a frozen progress bar while your deadline ticks away, wondering why you dropped two grand on a laptop that feels slower than your old desktop setup." 3. Solve Position your video as the ultimate relief. Present the fix clearly, swiftly, and confidently. Tech Example: "In this video, I’m showing you three free macOS tweaks that instantly drop your CPU temps by 15 degrees and cut your render times in half." Why it works for tech creators Tech audiences don't care about specs in a vacuum; they care about what those specs do for their workflow. By framing your videos around real-world friction, you build instant empathy. You turn a boring tutorial or review into a must-watch solution. Stop rambling about clock speeds in your intros.
