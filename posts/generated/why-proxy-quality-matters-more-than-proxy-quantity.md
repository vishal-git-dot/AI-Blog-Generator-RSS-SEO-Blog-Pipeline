# Why Proxy Quality Matters More Than Proxy Quantity

## Overview

When building automation workflows, many people focus on how many IPs a proxy provider offers. But in real projects, IP reputation, connection consistency, and location accuracy often matter more. A large IP pool doesn’t always mean better results. A smaller but cleaner and more reliable network can save a lot of time dealing with failed requests, blocks, and unstable sessions. Recently, I’ve been exploring different proxy solutions for AI-related data tasks and web automation. One provider I tested was Helodata, mainly because they focus on residential and ISP proxy infrastructure rather than just selling large IP numbers. Still comparing different providers, but the biggest lesson so far is that proxy performance should be measured by actual success rate, not only by marketing numbers
