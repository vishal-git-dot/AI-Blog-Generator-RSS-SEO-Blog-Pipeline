Step 1: Connect your calendars TidyCal syncs with Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar and iCloud Calendar, with up to 10 integrated calendars on either plan. Step 2: Set availability, buffers and limits Under availability, set: Working hours for when calls can be booked Buffer time between meetings so a call that runs over does not eat the next one Booking limits so a single day does not fill with calls when there is code to ship These three settings matter more than anything else for protecting focus time. Step 3: Create your booking types A sensible starting set: Discovery call — free, 20 minutes, one-on-one, Google Meet or Zoom link attached Paid consultation — 60 minutes, one-on-one, payment required Retainer check-in — recurring weekly booking for ongoing clients Workshop — group booking for a small cohort TidyCal allows unlimited booking types and can attach Zoom, Google Meet or Teams links. Step 5: Make the page and emails yours Pick a page style, add your logo and colors, and write custom confirmation and reminder emails.
