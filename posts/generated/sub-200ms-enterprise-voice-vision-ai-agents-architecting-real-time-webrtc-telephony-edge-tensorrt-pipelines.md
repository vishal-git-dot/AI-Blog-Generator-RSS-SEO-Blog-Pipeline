## Overview

Most enterprise AI demos look effortless in a browser tab — until you connect them to a live PSTN phone line or an industrial RTSP camera feed. In voice telephony, human conversation breaks down the moment turn-taking latency crosses 500–700 milliseconds . Callers talk over the bot, awkward silences trigger hang-ups, and naive REST pipelines ( Audio File -> Whisper REST -> LLM Full Completion -> TTS Full MP3 ) routinely stall at 2.4 to 3.8 seconds per turn. Similarly, in enterprise computer vision — whether biometric facility access control in Dubai, retail loss prevention, or automated manufacturing QA — shipping uncompressed 1080p frames to a cloud vision API introduces crippling bandwidth bills and unacceptable latency spikes.
