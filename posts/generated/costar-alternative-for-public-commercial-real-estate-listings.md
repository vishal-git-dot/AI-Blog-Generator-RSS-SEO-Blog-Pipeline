# CoStar Alternative for Public Commercial Real Estate Listings

## Overview

If you need public on-market commercial real estate listings in a spreadsheet or API, KazKN's LoopNet + Crexi Scraper is a practical CoStar alternative. It searches both platforms, merges the results, removes cross-platform duplicates, and normalizes price, size, and cap rate for $18 per 1,000 results. It is not a replacement for CoStar's full enterprise and private-data feature set; it is for brokers, acquisition teams, and analysts who need exportable public listings. When this CoStar alternative fits The best fit is a team that repeatedly rebuilds a market spreadsheet from public listings. A small broker might need current asking prices and broker details for one city. An acquisition analyst might need building size, NOI, cap rate, and days on market for a weekly review. In those cases, paying for each returned listing can be a better match than buying a broad enterprise system. The tool covers public on-market inventory from LoopNet and Crexi, not private or off-market ownership data. This actor searches LoopNet and Crexi, selects the most complete record when it identifies a cross-platform match, and marks the other platform in also_listed_on .
