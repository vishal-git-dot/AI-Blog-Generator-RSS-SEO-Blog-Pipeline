# Palantir's revenue jumped to $2,31 billion in the third quarter

## Overview

Palantir's Q3 2026 report shows total revenue up 96% year over year to $2.31 billion, driven by enterprise demand for secure frontier and open-weight model AI software. U.S. commercial revenue surpassed U.S. government revenue for the first time. The financial figures of the quarter The company's total revenue reached $2,31 billion, a 96% increase compared to the same period last year. Compared to the previous quarter, revenue increased by 20%, exceeding analysts' estimates of $2,18 billion. Earnings per share were $0,44, higher than the $0,41 analysts had expected. For the first time in the company’s history, revenue from commercial contracts in the United States exceeded government revenue. Specifically, US commercial contracts generated $984 million, up 148% from last year, while government revenue in the country amounted to $953 million for the same period.
