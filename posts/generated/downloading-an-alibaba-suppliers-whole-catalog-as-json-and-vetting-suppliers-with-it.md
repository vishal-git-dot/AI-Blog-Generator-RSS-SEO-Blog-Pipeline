# Downloading an Alibaba supplier's whole catalog as JSON (and vetting suppliers with it)

## Overview

I built this actor; it's a paid tool on Apify with a free trial credit. Searching Alibaba shows you products from hundreds of suppliers. Once you have a shortlist, the useful question flips: what else does this supplier sell, how focused is the catalog, which of their products actually sell, what is certified, and is the company behind it a 140-person factory or an 8-person trading office? That answer lives on the supplier's storefront ( name.en.alibaba.com ), 16 products per page, often 30 to 80 pages deep. Copying that by hand gets old fast, so I wrote Alibaba Supplier Store Scraper to turn a store URL, a product URL or a keyword into one clean JSON row per product, over plain HTTP. This post covers how it works, calling it from Python and Node, what a row looks like, and a small project: scoring suppliers from their catalogs. How it works A supplier storefront page server-renders its modules as URL-encoded JSON inside module-data attributes: the supplier card, the store's own category tree, and the product list with paging. The actor decodes those, maps each product card to flat fields (price range, MOQ, certificates, sold in the last 180 days, orders, ready-to-ship, store category with its parent) and copies the supplier card onto every row: business type, supplier identity, years on Alibaba, verified staff count, store rating, the inspection company that verified it, ranking badges, main products and capability tags such as "ODM services available" or "Patents awarded (6)". No contact person names, phones or emails are collected. After page 1 it knows the product count, so it queues exactly the pages it needs and reads them in parallel. Give it a product URL and it finds the supplier on the product page first; give it a keyword and it uses the JSON endpoint Alibaba's own search page calls to collect the suppliers behind the results. A keyword inside each store, ready-to-ship only, and price / MOQ filters are applied before anything is charged. It connects directly and only retries blocked requests through a proxy.
