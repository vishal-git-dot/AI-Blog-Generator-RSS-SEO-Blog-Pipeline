# AWS SAP-C03 and DVA-C03: What We Know So Far (GenAI and Agents Are In)

## Overview

AWS has announced revisions to two of its most popular certifications: Solutions Architect – Professional (SAP-C02 → SAP-C03) and Developer – Associate (DVA-C02 → DVA-C03) . This article collects what is publicly known right now, from the official blog post and the certification pages. Neither exam guide is published yet. The official blog states that the full exam guide, including detailed task statements, will be released on October 27, 2026, when registration opens. Everything below is based solely on the official blog (English and Japanese editions) and the certification pages. I'll update this article once the guides are out. 1. SAP-C03 (Solutions Architect – Professional) Dates and format Item Detail Registration opens October 27, 2026 (all languages) Last day to take SAP-C02 November 16 — see note below SAP-C03 available November 17 Duration 180 minutes Questions 75 Price 300 USD The format is unchanged from the current exam. Why the revision The blog states that today's architects "don't just design highly available, cost-effective systems — they integrate generative AI and agentic architectures and implement post-quantum cryptography." Domain structure The current four domains of SAP-C02 are reorganized into five: Design and implement cloud-native architectures Security, compliance, and governance Design cost-optimized architectures Resilience, migration, and business continuity Operational excellence and automation Domain weightings are not stated in the blog. Target candidate Two or more years of experience designing and implementing cloud solutions on AWS. Candidates are expected to evaluate requirements and provide guidance on cloud-native, serverless, and AI-integrated architectures. 2. Why the revision The blog positions AI-assisted development as "a core competency, not an optional add-on," and describes today's developers as using AI-assisted development tools to generate, review, and optimize code, and integrating managed AI services into applications.
