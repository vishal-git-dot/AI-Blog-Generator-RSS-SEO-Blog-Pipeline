Krutrim AI Developed by Ola, Krutrim is India’s first major indigenous AI model. Best for: Writing essays in regional languages, translating study materials, and summarizing textbook chapters in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and Marathi. Hanooman AI Backed by Reliance Industries and the BharatGPT consortium, Hanooman is built specifically to address the language barrier in Indian education. It supports voice-to-text and text-to-text operations across major Indian languages. Best for: Multilingual academic research and voice-based learning for students who prefer listening to explanations in their mother tongue. Pricing: Completely free public access for basic educational research. 3. Sarvam AI Sarvam AI focuses on developing high-performance, low-cost LLMs tailored for Indian use cases. Their models are optimized to run efficiently even on low-end smartphones with limited internet speeds, which is highly beneficial for students in rural India. Best for: Real-time speech translation of lectures and converting English text-heavy reference books into regional audio files. Pricing: Free open-source models; developer sandbox access is free for students. 4. Juggalbandi AI Supported by the government and developed in collaboration with AI4Bharat, Juggalbandi acts as a conversational guide. While initially designed to help rural citizens understand government welfare policies, it is incredibly useful for students researching government scholarships, education loans, and competitive exam rules. Best for: Finding verified information about state and central education schemes through simple WhatsApp voice notes. Pricing: Free public service. 5. AskGita AskGita is an specialized AI system trained on ancient Indian texts to provide mental wellness guidance, stress management, and moral clarity. For students experiencing exam anxiety or academic burnout, it offers a constructive way to process stress. Best for: Mindset coaching, dealing with exam pressure, and finding focus strategies rooted in Indian philosophy. Pricing: Free.
