Why Fine-Tuning Alone Fails at Macro-Reasoning In Part 2 , we showed that when an 8B specialist model encounters missing variables in a messy financial filing, standard RLHF conditions it to panic: it grabs loosely related numbers from adjacent rows to satisfy the prompt, falling directly into the Compensation Hallucination Trap . We fixed this at the micro-level with response-only masking and rejection circuit breakers . But in enterprise finance, you quickly encounter a macro-level barrier: Imagine handing an autonomous agent an audited annual report (like the official French standalone filing — comptes sociaux — of Renault SAS , Renault Group's main operating subsidiary) and asking: "Is a leveraged buyout (LBO) of this target financially feasible based on its latest audited accounts?" Answering this is not a one-shot retrieval problem. It requires navigating across multiple disparate accounting schedules: Finding the operating subsidiary's Self-Financing Capacity ( CAPACITE D'AUTOFINANCEMENT , a proxy for pre-CapEx operating cash flow in French GAAP) in the Cash Flow Statement ( Tableau de Financement ). Cross-referencing current Financial Debt in Note E.4 of the balance sheet annex. Calculating the Debt-to-Cash Flow repayment horizon ( capacité de remboursement , a core French banking covenant using CAF as a pre-CapEx cash-flow proxy for EBITDA). Pulling Equity from Note E.1 to calculate balance sheet Gearing. Computing the operating margin buffer from the standalone Income Statement of Renault SAS ( RESULTAT D'EXPLOITATION / CHIFFRE D'AFFAIRES ). If you shove all these tables into a single context window and ask an 8B model to reason through all five steps at once, attention anxiety returns with a vengeance . The model conflates standalone operating subsidiary debt with consolidated group figures, divides equity by operating profit, or produces subtle, catastrophic semantic inversions. To solve this, we must enforce Separation of Concerns at the system architecture level. 2. The GenRM Critic: Adversarial Auditing Before C-Suite Delivery Most agent frameworks rely on the LLM's own self-critique (e.g., "Review your previous response and fix any errors" ). As research has repeatedly demonstrated, this triggers sycophancy and confirmation bias : the model rationalizes its mistakes rather than catching them. What does GenRM stand for? GenRM stands for Generative Reward Model —an adversarial verifier pattern introduced in Generative Verifiers: Reward Modeling as Next-Token Prediction (arXiv:2408.15240) .
