Open innovation gives developers more freedom to experiment, customise, and build technology around real-world needs. For an event-planning application, local AI can offer useful advantages. Event details may include personal preferences, budgets, and locations. When inference runs locally, these details can remain on the user's machine instead of being sent to an external AI inference provider. Open-weight models can also offer greater control over model selection and help reduce recurring API costs. Local inference still requires suitable hardware, and offline operation depends on having the model and necessary resources available locally. For me, open innovation is about control, flexibility, and accessibility.
