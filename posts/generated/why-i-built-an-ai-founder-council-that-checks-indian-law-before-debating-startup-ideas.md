# Why I built an AI founder council that checks Indian law before debating startup ideas

## Overview

Most AI startup validators act like Silicon Valley cheerleaders. You feed them an idea for an on-demand laundry app or a niche micro-SaaS, and they immediately spit out: "Great idea! Here is a 5-step roadmap to build an MVP and talk to 100 users." If you try building that in India, that generic advice is usually where founders bleed runway. In India: People don't pay for software the same way (India 1 has purchasing power; India 2 and 3 expect everything to be free or WhatsApp-based). Collection friction kills margin (UPI recurring mandate drop-offs, payment gateway compliance). Regulation is not an afterthought — if your model touches lending, gaming, crypto, or health data, building an MVP without checking RBI/GST/IT rules can get your bank accounts frozen before your first customer signs up. To solve this, I built Baithak : an open-source Claude Code skill that acts as an Indian founder council, enforcing an automated Indian legal gate before five simulated founder personas debate the idea. 🏛️ How Baithak Works: The Architecture Baithak is structured around a two-phase architecture: an objective Chair Gatekeeper , followed by a Multi-Seat Deliberation Council .`` 🔗 Open Source & Demo Live Interactive Demo: cipher-cmd.github.io/Baithak GitHub Repository: github.com/cipher-cmd/Baithak
