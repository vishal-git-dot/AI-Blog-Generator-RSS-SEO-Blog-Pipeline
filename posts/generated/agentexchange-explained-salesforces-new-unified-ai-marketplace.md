# AgentExchange Explained: Salesforce's New Unified AI Marketplace

## Overview

Salesforce just rebranded AppExchange into something much bigger, meet AgentExchange. If you've worked with Salesforce, you know AppExchange (apps), Slack Marketplace (integrations), and the growing Agentforce ecosystem (AI agents) used to live in separate places. Salesforce just merged all three into a single, unified marketplace. What's actually new: One searchable hub for apps, AI agents, and Slack integrations, no more jumping between marketplaces Built-in security reviews for every listing, so admins get more confidence before installing A smoother "Private Offers" flow for buying and negotiating enterprise deals Launched with 200+ partners on day one, including Google Cloud, Box, and DocuSign Why it matters: As more businesses adopt Agentforce and AI agents inside their Salesforce org, having a single, vetted place to discover and install both traditional apps and AI agents removes a lot of friction, especially for admins who previously had to evaluate security and compatibility across multiple disconnected marketplaces. If you're a Salesforce admin, developer, or ISV partner, this is worth understanding now. It's likely to become the default distribution channel going forward.
