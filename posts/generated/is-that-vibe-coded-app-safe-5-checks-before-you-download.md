# Is that vibe coded app safe? 5 checks before you download

## Overview

The rise of "vibe coding"—using AI tools to build software via natural language prompts—has lowered entry barriers for development but introduced significant security risks. With a majority of developers now adopting AI, many novice creators lack the technical expertise to identify critical bugs such as hardcoded secrets, missing input validation, and weak encryption. This trend often prioritizes functional aesthetics over secure coding practices, potentially exposing users to data breaches and prompt injection attacks. Recent incidents involving AI-driven platforms demonstrate the tangible dangers of unvetted code, where simple oversights can lead to critical vulnerabilities affecting thousands of users. To stay safe, it is essential to scrutinize developer reputations, evaluate app permissions, and understand the security model behind AI-generated tools.
