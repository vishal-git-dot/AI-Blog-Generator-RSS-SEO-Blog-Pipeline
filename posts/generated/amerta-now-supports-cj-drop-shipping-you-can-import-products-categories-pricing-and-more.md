# Amerta now supports CJ drop shipping, you can import products, categories, pricing and more.

## Overview

I wanted to share a major update on Amerta, a free and open-source headless e-commerce framework built natively on top of PayloadCMS. If you love the developer experience of Payload but want a structured, self-hosted foundation tailored specifically for e-commerce, that is exactly why I have been building Amerta. What is Amerta? Amerta turns your PayloadCMS backend into a production-ready storefront engine. Instead of fighting with monolithic setups or expensive subscription platforms, it gives you full database control, customizable collections, and headless freedom out of the box. The New Update: CJ Dropshipping Plugin Managing inventory manually via messy CSV uploads is a pain. To solve this, I just rolled out an official CJ Dropshipping plugin for the platform. Once you activate the plugin in your backend, you can seamlessly tie your Payload ecosystem directly to the supplier: Instant Product Imports: Pull items, high-res media galleries, and descriptions into your Payload collections in one click. Category Mapping: Automatically sync and structure nested supplier categories. Automated Pricing & Multi-Variant Sync: Dynamic sync loops update variations, stock numbers, and pricing schemas without manual intervention. The Tech Stack Core: PayloadCMS Database: MongoDB (Supported natively by Payload) Frontend Flexibility: Headless structure means you can hook it up to Next.js, Nuxt, SvelteKit, or whatever you prefer. Looking for Feedback This is a fully community-driven project, and it is 100% free to self-host. I would love for fellow developers to break it, test the sync pipelines, and share their thoughts on the architecture.
