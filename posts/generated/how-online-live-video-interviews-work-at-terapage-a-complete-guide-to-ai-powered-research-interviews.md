## Overview

T he world is moving faster than ever, and almost every field of work is finding ways to integrate artificial intelligence into its system. Qualitative researchers are now leaning on it too, for something that’s always been considered a deeply human skill: the interview. Video and in-person interviews have always sat at the core of qualitative research. They are pivotal to understanding what people think and feel at a grassroots level, something no survey can fully replace. But even this old, human method hasn’t stayed untouched by AI. Think about how a live interview used to work. Notes were jotted down by hand or typed into a document only the moderator could see. Transcriptions came back weeks later, and analysis happened on a completely different computer, often days after the actual conversation. Everything was in a different place, scattered across different tools and different people, and by the time anyone sat down to pull it all together, something had usually gone missing along the way. AI-powered Live Video Interviews have changed that entirely. Terapage’s version of this isn’t just a video call with a research label on it. It’s built so that AI is quietly at work in the background the whole time, before the interview, during it, and after, so nothing important is left depending on someone’s memory or a transcript nobody’s gotten around to. What are Live Video Interviews? Online live video interviews are real-time conversations conducted over the internet between a researcher and one or more participants. Unlike traditional face-to-face interviews, participants can join from anywhere using a secure video meeting platform. These interviews help researchers understand: Customer experiences User behaviours Product feedback Employee opinions Healthcare experiences Academic research Market trends Live video interviews retain the depth of face-to-face conversations while removing geographical limitations.
