SAP MM (Materials Management) is one of the most important modules of SAP ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning). It controls the activities involved in managing the supply chain, procurement/purchasing, and inventory management of an organization. Put in simpler terms, it assists organizations in answering queries such as which raw material is required, when it is required, how many units are required, from which vendor it should be purchased, and how many units are presently in the warehouse.
