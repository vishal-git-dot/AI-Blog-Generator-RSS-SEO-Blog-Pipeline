# Accountant vs CPA: Does the Difference Matter for Developers and Startups?

## Overview

For developers working as freelancers, running small software businesses, or building startups, accounting can become an important part of the business side of technology. One question that often comes up is: What is the difference between an accountant and a CPA, and which type of professional should a developer or startup consider? Accountants can work with financial records, bookkeeping, reporting, and other accounting tasks . CPAs generally have additional professional qualifications and may provide specialized accounting, tax, assurance, or advisory services depending on the jurisdiction. For a developer who is just starting a freelance business, basic bookkeeping and tax support might be the main concern. As the business grows, other needs can appear, such as financial reporting, business structure, tax planning, payroll, or compliance. There is also an interesting connection between software development and accounting. Developers often build systems that handle invoices, payments, expenses, subscriptions, payroll, and financial reporting.
