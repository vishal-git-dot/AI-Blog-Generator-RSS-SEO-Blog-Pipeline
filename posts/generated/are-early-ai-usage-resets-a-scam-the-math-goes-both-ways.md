# Are early AI usage resets a scam? The math goes both ways

## Overview

Video not showing? Watch the 18-second clip here. An early reset looks great on the usage screen: you're back at 100%. But it also starts a fresh seven-day clock. Your next normal refresh moves with it. That's the bit that makes this less obvious than “free quota = good.” How much could you actually use with the reset versus without it? Two cases, same usage pace Both cases use a Pro-style plan and the same person using 250% of a weekly allowance per week, across 16 active hours a day. The subscription keeps going. The only things that change are the balance at reset time and when the next ordinary refresh would have happened. At reset time Net result Why 10% left; normal refresh 4 days 2 hours away +49% of a weekly allowance The refill comes early enough to use before the delayed refresh hurts much. 100% left; normal refresh 2 days away −42.9% of a weekly allowance There's nothing to refill, but the next refresh is pushed from 2 days away to 7 days away. The second one is the trap. The screen still says 100% right after the reset. You haven't lost quota right then . What you lost is the refresh that was two days away. How the comparison works Run the next stretch of time twice: once with the early reset and once without. In each run, usage follows the same pace, stops when the allowance is exhausted, and resumes when that run's weekly refresh arrives. Subtract total usable work in the two runs. That's the percentage above. The clip also shows +$27.05 and −$23.68 . Those are usage equivalents based on an illustrative $240 monthly payment, not money refunded or charged. The $240 example is a $200 plan with 20% tax added; actual tax varies. Your remaining balance, time until the ordinary refresh, and usage pace do most of the work here.
