# Grammarly ads by channel: AI in 27 of 30 LinkedIn texts, 0 of 30 on Meta

## Overview

The searches around Grammarly are mostly about AI. I wanted to see whether its ads use the same word, so I ran one regular expression over what my collector kept on October 30, 2026: Meta and LinkedIn ad texts, decoded Google headlines and Google Trends queries. The split is sharp. 27 of 30 LinkedIn ad texts mention AI. 0 of 30 Meta ad texts do. On the search side, 7 of the 10 rising queries are about AI humanizers or AI detectors. Sources and what the collector keeps Four public sources, one run each: Meta Ad Library , the Grammarly Facebook page: 150 ads, all 150 active.
