[INTERNAL:geothermal-energy|Exploring Geothermal Technologies] How Does Geothermal Drilling Work? The process involves several key stages: Site selection and surveying : Identifying suitable locations with high geothermal potential. Drilling : Utilizing specialized rigs to penetrate the Earth’s crust to reach superheated rocks. Heat extraction : Pumping water into the drilled wells, where it is heated by the surrounding rocks, and then brought back to the surface to generate steam. Electricity generation : The steam drives turbines that produce electricity, which can then be fed into the grid. This method not only provides a consistent energy source but also minimizes greenhouse gas emissions, making it a viable alternative to fossil fuels.
