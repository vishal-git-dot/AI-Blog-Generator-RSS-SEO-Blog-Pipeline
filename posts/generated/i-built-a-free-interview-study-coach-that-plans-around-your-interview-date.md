# I built a free interview study coach that plans around your interview date

## Overview

I'm building a small tool for people with a fixed software-engineering interview date and only a few hours a week. You give it the date, your level and your hours. It then: plans the weeks up to the interview, schedules coding practice by pattern, with spaced repetition, runs each problem as a timed session with the statement, an editor and a whiteboard on one screen, makes system design something you practise on a board rather than only read about. It is free. It is plain ES modules with no framework and no build step. There are no prices and no paid tier. Try it with made-up data, no account: https://jeremyahflowers.github.io/interview-prep-coach/demo/ The demo uses an invented persona and keeps everything in memory. Reload to reset it. The real app: https://jeremyahflowers.github.io/interview-prep-coach/ The page is titled "Ledger", which is the app's working name. Your data stays with you: on your device or, if you choose, in a GitHub repo you own (that option needs a token you create). Code: https://github.com/JeremyahFlowers/interview-prep-coach It is a working prototype used daily by one person, so expect rough edges.
