# Small Company Rides Anthropic Hype With A $657K AI Bet, Not $800M

## Overview

Genius Group , a small education company listed in New York, is telling shareholders that Anthropic is now the biggest holding in its AI investment pot after Anthropic's record IPO. The catch: the only purchase it has disclosed for that pot so far is $657,100, set against a headline plan of $800 million. Key Takeaways Genius Group says Anthropic now makes up 21.3% of its AI Treasury, held indirectly through two publicly traded funds Its only disclosed AI Treasury purchase so far is $657,100, made in June 2026, while its plan targets up to $800 million over five years The biggest indirect holdings are Anthropic at 21.3%, OpenAI at 12.4%, SpaceX at 7.3% and Databricks at 4.1% The company says its shares trade at a fraction of their asset value and plans to fund the treasury with preferred shares The global education group Genius Group announced the update one day after Anthropic , the frontier AI laboratory behind the Claude family of AI models, began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market following an initial public offering priced at $83 per share that raised about $81.3 billion. On May 27, its board approved the treasury with a plan to deploy up to US$100 million of initial capital as the first phase of a US$800 million, five-year investment program into companies leading the development of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). AI Treasury By The Numbers Genius Group said its AGI Infinity Portfolio is structured to give shareholders look-through exposure to frontier AI companies through Securities and Exchange Commission registered, publicly traded closed-end funds. The first step was a US$657,100 investment made in June 2026 in Destiny Tech100 and Fundrise Innovation Fund. Anthropic joins SpaceX as the second AI Treasury look-through holding to go public since those first investments. Genius Group also said the treasury includes pre-IPO exposure to OpenAI , Databricks and Anduril . The company said Anthropic was then valued at $965 billion in its May 2026 Series H funding round and about $1.5 trillion at the IPO, a 295% increase from the valuation at the time of the investment. For SpaceX, Genius Group said the June 12 listing valued it at about $1.77 trillion, and its market capitalization was about $1.95 trillion based on its Nov. 18 closing share price. Over the past two years, small listed companies have bought Bitcoin to tie their share price to a hot asset. Now some are doing the same with the private AI giants, buying funds that hold slices of companies like Anthropic, OpenAI and SpaceX before they list. Genius Group does both: its Aug. 27 capital plan covered an AI Treasury and a Bitcoin Treasury. The numbers deserve a close look. A 21.3% weighting sounds big, but it is a share of a small pot. Genius Group's disclosed spending so far is $657,100, split between Destiny Tech100 and Fundrise Innovation Fund, so its indirect Anthropic exposure is a fraction of that. Funds like these can also trade above or below the value of what they hold, which adds another layer of risk for anyone trying to ride the AI boom through them. Roger James Hamilton , Genius Group's chief executive, said Anthropic and SpaceX are now trading on Nasdaq "at a combined valuation of over $3 trillion." He also said Genius Group "has recently been trading at a share price which is at a fraction of its Net Asset Value Per Share," a sign the company hopes the AI link will lift its own stock. Genius Group describes itself as an AI-powered education group serving 6 million users in more than 100 countries, and it says the treasury sits alongside that business. What To Watch Next The first test is money. Genius Group said it plans to keep adding to the treasury and to pay for it with perpetual preferred shares rather than ordinary shares, as it announced on Aug. 27. Watch for whether it actually raises that capital and how much goes in. The second is Anthropic's share price. The company cited an average analyst price target of $116.38 for Anthropic, 40% above the IPO price, but warned that outside targets and forecasts are not its own and "may not be achieved," and that its treasury holdings could fall in value. Expect more of this. With Anthropic and SpaceX now public and OpenAI still private, more small companies may launch AI treasuries to catch investor attention.
