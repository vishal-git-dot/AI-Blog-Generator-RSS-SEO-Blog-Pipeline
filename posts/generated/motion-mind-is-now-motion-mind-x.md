# Motion Mind Is Now Motion Mind X

## Overview

Motion Mind Is Now Motion Mind X Today, I'm officially announcing a name change for my brand. Motion Mind is now Motion Mind X. 🌐 Official website: https://motionmindx.com Why Did I Change the Name? When I first started programming, I chose the name Motion Mind for my own brand. At that time, I didn't know that the name was already being used elsewhere. Over time, I introduced my projects under this name and continued building my identity around it. Projects like LeveloJS and Kivex became part of the Motion Mind ecosystem. So, changing the entire name wasn't something I wanted to do. I wanted a name that felt more unique while keeping the identity I had already built. That's why I decided to change the name to Motion Mind X . I haven't come across another brand using this exact name so far, and I believe it gives my brand a more distinctive identity without losing its connection to the original Motion Mind. What Changes Now? The official brand name is now Motion Mind X . However, my nickname, MotionMind2007 , which I've used across different platforms, will remain unchanged. My existing projects and the identity I've built around Motion Mind will continue under the new brand name. From now on, In-sha-Allah, you'll find my projects under Motion Mind X. A New Name, The Same Journey This isn't the beginning of a completely new brand. It's the next step for something I've already been building. I'm grateful to everyone who has followed my journey so far, and I hope you'll continue being part of it. Thank you for being here. Welcome to Motion Mind X. 🌐 https://motionmindx.com
