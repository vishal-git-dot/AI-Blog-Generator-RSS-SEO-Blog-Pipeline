# How to Sync Shopify Orders into Odoo Automatically with Python & Webhooks

## Overview

Running a growing e-commerce brand on Shopify while managing inventory, fulfillment, and accounting in Odoo ERP is a very common architecture. However, many teams start by manually exporting CSVs from Shopify and importing them into Odoo at the end of every business day. As order volume scales, manual entry creates bottlenecks: delayed fulfillment, stockouts due to delayed inventory updates, human entry errors, and missing customer data. In this guide, I will walk you through how to build a clean, event-driven integration middleware in Python. When an order is placed on Shopify, a webhook triggers a lightweight Python service that verifies the payload, maps the data, and automatically creates a confirmed Sales Order in Odoo via XML-RPC.
