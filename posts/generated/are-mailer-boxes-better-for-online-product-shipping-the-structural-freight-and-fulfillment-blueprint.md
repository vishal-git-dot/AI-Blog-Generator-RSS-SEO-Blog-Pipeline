Core Baseline Headings Heading 2A: Structural Comparison: Roll End Tuck Top (RETT) Mailers vs. Regular Slotted Cartons (RSCs) Navigating e-commerce packaging requires understanding the structural mechanics that separate custom mailers from traditional industrial shipping boxes. STRUCTURAL ARCHITECTURE COMPARISON ROLL END TUCK TOP (RETT) REGULAR SLOTTED CARTON (RSC) +------------------------------+ +------------------------------+ | Single-piece clamshell dieline| | Four-flap slotted container | | Double-walled side panels | | Requires top/bottom tape | | Friction-fit front tuck tabs | | Industrial bulk stacking | +------------------------------+ +------------------------------+ Roll End Tuck Top (RETT) Mailer Architecture The RETT mailer is a single-piece, self-locking clamshell container engineered with double-walled side panels, internal dust flaps, and front friction tabs. When folded, the side panels roll inward to create double-thickness sidewalls that absorb lateral compression. The front lid locks securely into the base frame without requiring exterior packing tape, creating a clean outer surface and an unboxing reveal when opened. Regular Slotted Carton (RSC) Shipping Box Architecture The RSC is the traditional industrial workhorse of transport packaging. It features four top and bottom flaps of equal length that meet in the center during assembly. Because its closure flaps do not lock mechanically, RSC boxes require pressure-sensitive adhesive tape or mechanical stitching across both top and bottom seams to secure the contents. Assembly Speed & Labor Effort Analysis Fulfillment velocity depends heavily on box architecture. Pre-scored fold lines and friction-lock tabs on RETT mailers accelerate manual assembly, allowing packers to square, pack, and lock a mailer in seconds. In contrast, RSC shippers require a multi-step process: squaring the box, taping the bottom flaps, inserting the product and void fill, and applying top sealing tape—doubling labor time. Tamper Evidence and Security RETT Mailers: Secure contents through interlocking side flaps and tuck tabs. While they offer a clean unboxing experience, they do not provide raw tamper-evident seals unless secured with a branded sticker or security label. RSC Shippers: Tape-sealed seams provide immediate visual tamper evidence across parcel transit hubs, making them ideal for high-value industrial goods. Corrugated Strength Ratings Demystified The structural capacity of corrugated board is measured using the Edge Crush Test (ECT) , which evaluates the force ($\text{lb/in}$) required to crush a vertical section of board on its edge: 32 ECT Single-Wall: Standard industry rating for custom D2C mailers shipping contents up to $30\text{ lbs}$. 44 to 55 ECT Single-Wall / Double-Wall: Required for heavy industrial RSC shippers and multi-tier palleted warehouse storage. Void Fill and Tare Weight Impact Oversized stock RSC shippers frequently force operations to pack items in excessive void fill (packing peanuts, crumpled kraft paper, or air pillows) to prevent shifting. This excess void fill increases tare weight and inflates package volume. Custom mailers hug exact product dimensions tightly, eliminating loose void fill and lowering shipping costs. 3.
