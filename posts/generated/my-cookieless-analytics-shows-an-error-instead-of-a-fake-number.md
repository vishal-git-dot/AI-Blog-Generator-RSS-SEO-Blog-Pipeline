# my cookieless analytics shows an error instead of a fake number

## Overview

most dashboards would rather show you a confident zero than admit they don't know. mine isn't allowed to. i'm om yaduvanshi, 18, an applied ai developer building cloudline , a cookieless analytics tool. day 16 of 90, building in public. the day my dashboard lied to me early on i opened my own dashboard and found a dead day. no visits, flat zero, and the chart drew it with total confidence. it looked exactly like a slow day, so i almost read it as one. the cause was boring. a deploy had missed the script tag. the scary part wasn't the bug, it was the presentation. nothing on the screen suggested anything was wrong. a missing day and a slow day rendered identically, and i was one distracted glance away from building a story on air. zeros are the most expensive lie in analytics a wrong number still gets quoted. "traffic dropped 40% last week" becomes a decision before anyone checks whether last week was real. most dashboards keep the friendly zero anyway, because a dashboard that shows errors looks broken and a dashboard that shows zeros looks fine. i went the other way. when cloudline can't trust its data, it says so. high bursts may get throttled, and when they do you see the message, not a chart that pretends everything is normal. a filter with no matching visits says "no data", not zero. a deleted site takes its data with it immediately, so no ghost rows from dead properties linger in your totals. the honest-limit part this costs me. every error message is a support question a friendly zero would have avoided. "why does my dashboard say this" is a worse first impression than a calm chart. i know that. i also know the calm chart is the thing that lets you believe a lie. the site's own limits page says it in one sentence: errors show a clear message instead of wrong numbers. that's the whole design philosophy. a dashboard's job is to be trusted on the days it has nothing good to say. that's the day you learn whether it deserves you. free while in early access. no card. https://trycloudline.space — om
