# Why a "$75/hr" freelance rate is often $34/hr net (so I built a free client-side calculator to fix it)

## Overview

One of the most expensive mistakes independent developers, designers, and solo consultants make is pricing projects using "salary math" ( $100,000 / 2,000 hours = $50/hr ). In reality, three silent leaks eat freelance margins alive: Billable Utilization (~55–65%): Between scoping, debugging client environments, proposals, admin, and context switching, most solo operators only bill 22–25 hours out of a 40-hour desk week. Self-Employment Taxes & Overhead: Setting aside 25–30% for taxes plus annual SaaS/hardware burn cuts raw hourly numbers dramatically. Reverse Payment Fees & Uncapped Revisions: Charging $2,500 via international PayPal, Stripe FX, or Upwork doesn't net $2,500 — unless you calculate the reverse gross invoice ( (Net + Fixed Fee) / (1 - % Fee) ). And two "quick weekend tweaks" without a revision cap quietly drop your effective hourly rate by 30%.
