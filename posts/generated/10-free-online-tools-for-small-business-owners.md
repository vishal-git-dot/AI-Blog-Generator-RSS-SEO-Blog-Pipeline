How do I send invoices and receipts without paid software? How do I make product photos look professional for free? Good product photos come from three small steps: the right size, a small file, and your name on the image. Resize first with the Image Resizer so the photo matches the size it will actually be shown at, then shrink the file with the Image Compressor . How do I keep business accounts secure? Use a different strong password for every business account, and never reuse the password from your personal email. One reused password is the simplest way for a single leak to open your shop email, your marketplace account and your payment dashboard at once. The Password Generator creates long, random passwords you do not have to invent yourself, and because it runs in your browser, the passwords it makes are never sent anywhere. Store them in a password manager, not in a notes app or a spreadsheet, and turn on two-factor authentication on every account that offers it — especially email, since email resets unlock almost everything else.
