# Daytona surpasses $1 billion valuation

## Overview

Daytona has raised a $60 million Series B round led by FirstMark Growth, pushing its valuation to $1 billion. The three-year-old startup provides isolated developer environments running on Linux, Windows, macOS, and GPUs, tailored specifically for long-running AI workloads. The funding round was led by FirstMark Growth, bringing the total funding Daytona has raised since its founding three years ago to $91 million. FirstMark led its previous $24 million funding round in February. Investors and the leadership team The new funding round was led by well-known tech executives including Patrick Collison, Tobi Lütke, Aravind Srinivas, Eric Yuan, Olivier Pomel, Adam D'Angelo, and Brendan Foody. The investment teams of Qualcomm, Samsung, and Okta also participated. The company is headquartered in New York. Founder and CEO is Ivan Burazin, while the leadership team also includes architect Goran Draganic and technical director Vedran Jukic.
