# farmAI

## Overview

I built FarmAI, an AI assistant for farmers, and here’s the full demo. 🌾 Farmers in The Gambia often lose crops to diseases they spot too late, and good advice isn’t always within reach. So I built FarmAI to put it in their hands. What it does: 🌿 Disease detection: upload a photo of a crop leaf and get a diagnosis 🌱 Crop recommendation: find out what suits your soil and conditions 🌦️ Weather: forecasts to plan planting and harvesting 📈 Yield prediction: estimate what your harvest could look like 💬 AI chat (Gemini): ask farming questions in plain language 📲 SMS alerts: reach farmers on basic phones through Africa’s Talking 🗂️ History: keep track of past scans and advice Built with: Python, PyTorch, Streamlit, Gemini API, Africa’s Talking 📺 Watch the full demo: [ https://youtu.be/fXQQjLt-ZT0?si=bm1uaDCRyVJq7Lsr ] I’m building tools like this to solve real problems around me, and I’d love your feedback.
