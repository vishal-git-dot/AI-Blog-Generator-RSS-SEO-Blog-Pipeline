# Claude Can Now Track Every Attendee And Booth At Big Conferences, RainFocus Says

## Overview

RainFocus has launched a connector that lets Anthropic 's Claude look up attendees, check room capacity and track booths live at conferences run on its platform, with no spreadsheet exports needed. Key Takeaways The Anthropic-verified connector gives Claude live access to RainFocus event data across 17 areas Claude can look up attendees and speakers, check session and room capacity, and monitor on-site check-ins Claude can also create or update meetings, but only if a RainFocus admin turns on each write action The connector runs through MCP Profiles, the secure AI link inside the RainFocus Nexus platform RainFocus announced the connector on Tuesday for large conferences, trade shows and field events. The Utah company sells an event marketing platform. What Claude Can Do At An Event RainFocus says event data usually reaches sales and marketing teams slowly, because someone has to export it by hand. With the connector, Claude can search attendee profiles by name, email or company, query session schedules and speaker lists, check room capacity, follow check-in status and review exhibitor and booth records during the event. The tools span 17 areas, including registration, sessions and content, meetings and one-on-one scheduling, and exhibitors and booths.
