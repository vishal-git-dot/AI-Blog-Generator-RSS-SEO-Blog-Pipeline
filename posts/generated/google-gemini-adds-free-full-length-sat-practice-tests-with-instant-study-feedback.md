# Google Gemini Adds Free Full-Length SAT Practice Tests With Instant Study Feedback

Google Gemini now lets users take free, full-length SAT practice tests directly in the Gemini app . The feature combines vetted test content from education partners including The Princeton Review with immediate scoring, skill-area feedback, answer explanations, and a customizable study plan. It is a notable expansion of Gemini's education tools because it moves the product beyond on-demand question answering into a more structured test-preparation experience. Google says users can begin by prompting Gemini with a request such as “I want to take a practice SAT test.” According to Google's official Workspace update on Gemini SAT practice tests , the capability is available to Gemini users, including Google Workspace customers, Workspace Individual subscribers, and people using personal Google accounts signed into the app. For students, the central appeal is straightforward: a full exam can be accessed on demand at no cost, followed by feedback intended to identify where further study may be useful.
