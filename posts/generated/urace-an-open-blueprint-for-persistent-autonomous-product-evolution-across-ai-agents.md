# URACE — an open blueprint for persistent autonomous Product evolution across AI agents

## Overview

URACE (Universal Recursive Autonomous Co-Founder Engine) is an open, implementation-agnostic blueprint for persistent, resource-efficient autonomous Product evolution. It turns retained Intent into a continuous, measurable lifecycle that survives prompts, sessions, agents, models, and runtimes. Agents execute bounded work. URACE governs the continuous Product lifecycle across them. URACE.md is both the blueprint and AI bootstrap/install file: give it to a capable AI with your Product context to instantiate an environment-appropriate URACE implementation. The architecture is independent of any specific AI, vendor, language, or platform. MIT licensed and actively maintained. Technical feedback, issues, pull requests, and contributions are welcome. https://github.com/joaoalbertocsjunior/URACE
