# I Tested the Viral AI 3D Web Games on X: Here Is the Stack, GPU Reality, and What Prompts Still Can't Do

## Overview

Let's be honest: most "AI-generated game" clips on X look amazing for five seconds and fall apart the moment you actually try to play them. When a new wave of 3D browser games started racking up millions of views—right after GPT-6 Astra blew up 3D asset showcases and Claude Opus 5.5 started churning out procedural animations—I wanted to know what was actually running under the hood. On November 27, 2026, I dug through the 11 biggest viral builds from this wave, cloned the open-source repos ( turbo-kart-rally and voxel-musou ) onto an older laptop with an integrated Intel UHD 620 GPU, and tested the model APIs they used: ✅ Where this stack shines: Shipping a playable 3D browser prototype in 2 to 7 days with Three.js ⚠️ Where prompts alone fail: Branching RPG narratives, multiplayer state sync, and low-end GPU optimization Here are the 5 biggest engineering lessons I found after tearing these 11 projects apart. 🏎️ 1. "Zero External Assets" Does Not Mean "Zero GPU Load" One of the coolest open-source repos in this batch is an MIT-licensed Mario Kart clone ( turbo-kart-rally ) written in pure Three.js without a single build tool. The README states 100% procedural assets —downloading zero .gltf models and zero texture files: 🏁 8 procedural racers: Built out of Three.js geometric primitives and color palettes, each with custom speed, acceleration, handling, and weight stats 🏎️ Complete 100cc race loop: Connects the title screen, character select, 3-lap tracking ( LAP 1/3 ), item boxes, and Shift drift boosts Another developer pushed this procedural trick even further, packing an Underwater Shipwreck Exploration game (diving into a wreck to locate the ancient Antikythera mechanism) into a single 3MB HTML file —synthesizing deep-sea lighting, geometry, and underwater sound on the fly. People see "3MB file" or "no external models" and assume it runs on a potato. I benchmarked the kart racer on an Intel UHD 620 integrated GPU to test that theory: 🖥️ Browser hardware acceleration ON ( 800×450 ): Ran at ~39 FPS —completely playable, just slightly tight on sharp turns 🐢 Software rendering / GPU OFF ( 480×270 ): Collapsed to 1.5–6.5 FPS , making steering impossible Procedural code skips asset downloads and bundlers, not WebGL draw calls. To survive on low-end laptops, you still have to manually cut dynamic shadows, vehicle counts, and canvas resolution. 🎯 2. Every Working AI Game Secretly Uses the Same 5 Math Mechanics Why can an LLM one-shot a playable kart racer or turf shooter, yet choke on a narrative detective game? Look at the other browser builds in this batch: 🔫 #1 Viral Sci-Fi Squad Shooter: Wear heavy armor, push down urban streets with AI teammates, shoot enemy soldiers, and capture control points. Built with Claude Opus 5.5 and Three.js by a creator who never read a line of code—proving gunfire pacing, squad cover behavior, and capture feedback are human design calls. 🎨 Splatoon-Style Ink Painter (Claude Opus 5.5): Spray ink on the floor to claim territory in your team's color and dive into friendly ink to swim faster. 🌊 Voxel Water & Shader Test (Claude Opus 5.5): Classic blocky mountains and trees, upgraded with planar water reflections, sunbeams cutting through leaves, and day-to-night transitions. 🚀 Sky Reach Planetary Landing: Fly a spaceship toward a distant planet, punch through the atmosphere, land on the surface, and step out on foot. Strip away the paint and all 11 games rely on just five math-friendly mechanics : 🏃 Free movement + camera follow 🛤️ Path or waypoint progression 🟦 Grid-cell surface color changes 💥 Crowd collision & hitboxes 🔢 Procedural environment math None of them require branching dialogue trees, long-term economy balancing, or multiplayer netcode. LLMs shine when the core loop reduces to vectors, state machines, and distance checks. 🛠️ 3. Three.js, Tripo, Blender, and Unity Are Sequential—And You Can't Skip Blender Beginners constantly ask: "Should I use Three.js, Tripo, Blender, or Unity?" The creators who built character-heavy games chained them together across four distinct jobs: 🖼️ 2D Image Generation: Locks the character's visual look 🧊 Tripo: Converts that 2D image or text into a 3D mesh 🛠️ Blender: Fixes topology, rigging, and animations (the one step prompts cannot skip) 🎮 Unity or Three.js: Assembles the final game loop Here is how two viral builds used that pipeline: 🌹 The Little Prince 3D Planet (16 days, 3.46M views, ~10K bookmarks): Walk the Little Prince around his tiny planet to visit his rose and the king. Locking the 2D character art first before converting to 3D, cleaning up in Blender, and assembling in Unity made the book's cast recognizable immediately. 🕸️ Spider-Man City Swinging: Leap off rooftops, swing on webs above traffic and pedestrians, and run up building facades. After fixing the animations in Blender , the creator skipped Unity and exported straight back into a Three.js web page. ⚔️ 4. A Combat Room Takes 10 Minutes; A Game Loop Takes Human Choreography My favorite open-source repo in the list is Voxel Musou ( voxel-musou ), where you play as Zhao Yun slashing through crowds of voxel soldiers with combos, dodge rolls, and musou specials. The creator wrote that this was their childhood dream game. Version 1 was literally just Zhao Yun hitting soldiers in an empty space. What turned it into an actual game was the screen-by-screen flow they wired up afterward: 📜 Starting at the main menu and selecting Chapter 1 ( Hulao Gate · Normal ) 🛡️ Choosing Liu Bei, Guan Yu, or Zhang Fei on the Shu-Han route 🎙️ Watching a voiced cutscene with subtitles about Dong Zhuo and Yuan Shao's coalition 🎯 Entering the battlefield with a live 61m distance HUD ( Defeat Dong Zhuo's forces in front of Sishui Pass ) Even in the Call of Duty MW2 + Skate 3 + Minecraft mashup (7.5M views, ~30K likes—shoot MW2 guns, hit J to kickflip a skateboard, and shoot blocks in a Minecraft world), the magic is in the boundaries. Built in Rust on the open-source IW4L runtime, it keeps its repo clean by downloading ~125MB of vanilla Minecraft assets from Mojang's official servers on first launch, requiring a legal Steam copy of MW2, and disabling skateboarding if Xbox 360 Skate 3 's default.xex is missing. 🔌 5.
