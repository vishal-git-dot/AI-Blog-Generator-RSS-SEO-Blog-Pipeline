# PaieMinute: create a French payslip (bulletin de paie) in seconds

## Overview

I built PaieMinute, an online payslip tool for French small businesses (TPE and PME). You enter the employer, employee, contract and salary details. It calculates contributions, net pay and the withholding tax (prelevement a la source), then exports a PDF. The payslip is URSSAF 2026 compliant, costs 9.90 EUR per payslip, and has no subscription. Free tools on the site include a work certificate, an employment contract, a gross/net salary simulator, a mileage allowance calculator and an Excel template. Try it at paieminute.com .
