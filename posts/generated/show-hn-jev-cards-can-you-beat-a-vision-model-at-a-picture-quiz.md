# Show HN: Jev Cards, Can you beat a vision model at a picture quiz?

## Overview

I built a quiz game around TypeSafe's new Jev model, which has vision capabilities. Jev looks at a picture and a question and picks an answer (or taps a place) in about a quarter of a second. I've always loved quiz games and thought this would be a fun way to see what it can do and how fast it is. I made the cards from scratch, and Jev gets exactly the picture you see. More cards will come over time. Play against Jev on your own (there's a leaderboard for each mode), or start a live match and challenge your friends, with Jev as one more player. The code is open source, so feel free to build your own game or make more cards that I can publish.
