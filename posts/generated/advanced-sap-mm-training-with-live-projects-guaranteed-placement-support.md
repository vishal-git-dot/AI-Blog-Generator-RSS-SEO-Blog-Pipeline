Advanced mastery of Materials Management opens up global consulting assignments, remote international projects, and on-site client deployments. What is SAP MM? SAP MM (Materials Management) is a core functional module within the SAP ERP system. It manages everything related to the material lifecycle of an organization: Procurement: Managing the acquisition of raw materials, office supplies, or services from external vendors. Inventory Management: Tracking stock movements, managing warehouse locations, and handling goods receipts and issues. Purchasing Verification: Ensuring that incoming vendor invoices match purchase orders and delivery notes accurately. Vendor Master Data: Maintaining comprehensive records of suppliers, pricing agreements, and delivery timelines.
