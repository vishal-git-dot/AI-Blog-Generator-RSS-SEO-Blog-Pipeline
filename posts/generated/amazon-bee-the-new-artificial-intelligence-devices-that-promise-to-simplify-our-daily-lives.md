# Amazon Bee: The new artificial intelligence devices that promise to simplify our daily lives

## Overview

Amazon has introduced Bee, a new category of personal AI hardware designed for ambient computing. The lineup includes wearables and magnetic attachments for smartphones that utilize local sensor and AI processing to automate daily tasks. Technically, the architecture prioritizes privacy by processing audio in real-time and managing conversation data through client-side encryption, ensuring that transcripts remain under the user's control. What are Bee devices? The line includes small, wearable gadgets: one worn on the wrist or as a pendant and one that attaches magnetically to a mobile phone. The company's goal is what it calls "ambient computing." This means the devices work in the background, using sensors and artificial intelligence to automatically take on everyday tasks, without the user having to constantly open apps on their smartphone. According to Panay, the future of technology is not about us controlling apps, but about devices being able to understand and serve our needs automatically. Bee devices were designed to work in conjunction with the phone, helping the user stay focused on their activities. Privacy Amazon placed particular emphasis on the issue of privacy for this new series: Instant audio deletion: The devices process the audio in real time and delete it immediately afterwards. The original audio of the conversations is not stored on any system. Text encryption: The texts resulting from the conversations are locked with digital keys that are only on the user's device. Access security: Because of this locking method, no third party, not even Amazon employees, has the ability to read these files unless the user themselves chooses to share them.
