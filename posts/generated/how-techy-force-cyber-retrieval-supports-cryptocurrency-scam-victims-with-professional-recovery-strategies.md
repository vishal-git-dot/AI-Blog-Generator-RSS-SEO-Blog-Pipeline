Protect Yourself From Recovery Scams A trustworthy recovery service should never pressure you to surrender your private keys, seed phrase, passwords, or authentication codes. Be cautious of anyone demanding urgent payments for “unlocking,” “tax,” or “release” fees while guaranteeing that your funds will be returned. Before paying for any recovery service, verify the company, understand its fees and services in writing, and preserve all evidence related to your original loss.
