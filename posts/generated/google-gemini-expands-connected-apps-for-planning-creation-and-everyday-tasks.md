# Google Gemini Expands Connected Apps for Planning, Creation and Everyday Tasks

## Overview

Google has announced a new slate of connected apps and services for the Gemini App , expanding the assistant's ability to support planning, creation and task management from one conversational interface. The phased rollout adds named partners across productivity, local bookings, entertainment, music and lifestyle services. The central change is not a single new Gemini model feature. It is a broader set of app connections intended to let people move from asking Gemini for help to completing related actions, such as summarizing a meeting, editing a website, finding an experience or making a reservation. For businesses, the practical value will depend on which apps become available in their region and whether the connections fit existing work. What Google announced for Gemini connected apps In its official announcement of new connected apps and services for Gemini , Google said the additions would roll out over the following weeks. The company describes the experience as one where users can turn on favorite apps and services in Gemini to plan, create and tackle to-dos in one place. The announcement names a diverse set of partners. It therefore represents a meaningful broadening from Gemini's earlier, more limited connected-app announcements and Personal Intelligence integrations . Google has confirmed the partners and categories, but it has not provided a complete availability map, detailed activation instructions or pricing information for the expanded connections. Where the expansion could help everyday workflows For teams already using Gemini, the most useful starting point may be a workflow with a clear handoff between information and action.
