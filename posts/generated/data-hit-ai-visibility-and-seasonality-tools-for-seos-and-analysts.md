# Data Hit: AI visibility and seasonality tools for SEOs and analysts

## Overview

DATA HIT Tools is a set of five visual tools for SEOs, marketers and analysts. Two show how AI answers treat your brand: How much of your search demand Google now answers with AI, and whether those answers link to you; How often ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Mode name you, with a margin of error rather than a single screenshot. The other three turn search results and your GA4 or Search Console data into charts that show seasonality and timing a table flattens. Every tool has a free way in, and several load with sample data before you sign up.
