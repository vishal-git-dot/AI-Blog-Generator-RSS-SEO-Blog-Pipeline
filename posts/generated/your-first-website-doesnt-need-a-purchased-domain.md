# Your First Website Doesn't Need a Purchased Domain

## Overview

Every day, thousands of great side projects die before they start — not because of code, but because of a false assumption: " I need to buy a domain first. " You don't. For a portfolio, a college project, a community page, or a personal CV, a free subdomain does the job perfectly. What a subdomain actually is A subdomain is a prefix added to a base domain: yourname.ai.id . It gets its own DNS records, its own SSL certificate, and points wherever you want. Functionally, for visitors and for Google, it behaves like a real address — because it is one. How to get one free (under a minute) Register at AICLUB (email + OTP) Claim a name across 13 base domains (.ai.id, .web.id, .cv, and more) Add one DNS record: CNAME for Cloudflare Pages / Vercel / Netlify / GitHub Pages, or an A record for your own server TLS is automatic once DNS is live No ads, no credit card. One free subdomain per account. Why I built this I'm Yan, an indie builder from Indonesia. I kept seeing talented people stall on this exact step, so I built AICLUB — a free subdomain service running on Cloudflare. My own portfolio (yans.ai.id) runs on it too. While you're there: the site also hosts a curated catalog of 500+ free, open-source AI tools with live GitHub stats — useful for picking tools that are actually maintained. The honest trade-offs A subdomain is not a custom domain: for a serious long-term business, buying a domain eventually makes sense (it costs about two cups of coffee a year) Free services vary in trust: pick one with an abuse policy and no ads — spammy free hosts can hurt how search engines view your content Your content matters more than the address: a shipped project on a free subdomain beats a perfect idea on no domain at all Ship it first. Buy the domain when the project earns it.
