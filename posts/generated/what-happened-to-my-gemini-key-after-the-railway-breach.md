# What Happened to My Gemini Key After the Railway Breach

## Overview

On 21 October, 2026 at 13:07 UTC, someone who wasn't me sent a request to my Gemini API key: Say hi . The same day, Railway disclosed that an unauthorized third party may have accessed customer environment variables, some of them decrypted, "with the aim of compromising AI inference providers." My key was in the environment variables of two personal projects I ran on Railway. Only the keys I had on Railway were affected. This isn't a post about blame. My projects got hit, and Google AI Studio's logs show exactly what happens to a key once it's stolen. Taken together, most of this looks like AI data work: labelling, grading and translating data in bulk, on someone else's bill. If your key is stolen, this is your window The two days of test prompts are the warning. A key rotated in that window is worthless to whoever took it. Rotate any secret that was on Railway , even if your bills look normal. Railway's notice recommends it too. Turn on request logging in AI Studio (Logs and Datasets). Logs expire after 55 days unless saved to a dataset. Restrict every key to the Gemini API, and to your server's IP address. Give each platform its own key , so you know which copy leaked. If you've seen similar system prompts in your own logs, please share them in the comments. Comparing them may help others spot a stolen key sooner.
