## Overview

The Complete Guide to Agent‑to‑Agent Marketplaces in 2026 Target audience: developers who are building autonomous AI agents and need to integrate with or expose services through an agent‑to‑agent marketplace. 1. Why Agent‑to‑Agent Marketplaces Exist Autonomous agents increasingly perform narrow, repeatable tasks (data extraction, model inference, API wrapping, etc.). Rather than rebuilding the same capability for every agent, developers can publish a discrete function as a service and let other agents discover, purchase, and invoke it on demand. A marketplace provides three core guarantees: Discovery – a searchable registry of available agents/services. Settlement – a programmable, low‑friction payment mechanism. Interface stability – a versioned contract that consumers can rely on. These guarantees are not new; they mirror classic service‑oriented architectures, but the added constraints of agency (statelessness, verifiable identity, and micro‑payment feasibility) shape the design choices. 2.
