# AI is not the end of the technology cycle.

## Overview

It may be the beginning of the next one. The internet connected people to information. Cloud computing connected people to scalable computing. AI is making intelligence increasingly accessible. So what happens when intelligence becomes abundant? I believe the next major shift will be from AI that answers questions to systems that can independently operate, coordinate, transact and act in the real world. That opens the door to a much bigger technology stack: Autonomous agent networks Machine-to-machine economies Robotics and physical AI Synthetic biology + AI Autonomous scientific research Energy intelligence Digital identity and trust infrastructure Intelligence marketplaces Spatial computing Brain-computer interfaces The important part is that these technologies won't develop independently. They will converge. AI agents will use marketplaces. Agents will transact with other agents. Machines will interact with digital infrastructure. Robots will bring intelligence into the physical world. AI will accelerate scientific discovery. Synthetic biology will make parts of biology programmable. New identity and trust systems will become necessary when billions of autonomous actors enter the network. This could lead to something much bigger than today's AI ecosystem: The Autonomous Internet. An internet connecting not only people and information, but: People → AI → Agents → Machines → Organizations → Physical Systems And that changes the question. The opportunity may not be to build another AI model. It may be to build the infrastructure that allows millions of intelligent systems to work together safely and efficiently. Models provide intelligence. The next layer will provide context, memory, orchestration, verification, identity, permissions, marketplaces and real-world execution. That is the layer I believe is worth watching. AI may be the technology of this decade. But the systems built around autonomous intelligence could define the next one. What do you think comes after AI?
