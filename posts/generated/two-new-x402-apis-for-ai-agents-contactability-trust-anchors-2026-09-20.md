Categories scored together for a single contactability_score 0-100 with A-F grade: Email addresses — visible + mailto: + obfuscated [at] / [dot] reconstructed + HTML-entity deobfuscated ( &#64; , &commat; ). Role-bucketed into support/sales/info/contact/admin/no-reply/other. Phone numbers — NANP + E.164 formats, classified as toll-free (800/888/877/866/855/844/833) vs local. Physical addresses — JSON-LD PostalAddress detection, schema.org Organization.address , plus a visible-text heuristic that catches unmarketed street + city + state + zip blocks.
