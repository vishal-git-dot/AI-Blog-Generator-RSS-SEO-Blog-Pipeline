# Vozelle: AI voice changer for mobile chat apps

## Overview

What is Vozelle? Most voice changers force you to record audio, save the file, then manually upload it to chats. Vozelle is designed differently: it sits on top of your messaging apps and processes your voice on the fly. Once you pick an AI voice, any voice note you record through the floating overlay will be automatically converted, ready to send as a normal voice message. The voice library covers many styles: anime, movie, game characters, male and female tones, plus soft, deep and playful voices. You can preview your voice before sending, and recordings can be up to 5 minutes long. Supported Apps Vozelle works with these platforms for voice messages: WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Snapchat, Telegram, X, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp Business，Discord(Android). Important note: Vozelle only supports voice messages, not live voice calls. Feature availability varies a little between Android and iOS. Common Use Cases Roleplay & creative chats: speak as your favourite anime, game or movie character Dating and casual chats: feel more comfortable with a voice you prefer Fun with friends: surprise people with character voices Gender expression: explore different tones for your voice messages How it works (3 simple steps) Open Vozelle and select the AI voice you want to use Turn on the floating overlay widget Jump into your chat app, record, and send your changed voice note directly Official Website [ https://www.vozelle.ai/ ] You can download Vozelle for Android and iOS from our official site. Pricing A free version is available so you can test all the voices. Vozelle Pro removes limits on voice messages. New users can currently claim a 7-day free Pro trial. Rules & Disclaimer Please use Vozelle responsibly. Do not use it for impersonation, scams, harassment or any illegal activity.
