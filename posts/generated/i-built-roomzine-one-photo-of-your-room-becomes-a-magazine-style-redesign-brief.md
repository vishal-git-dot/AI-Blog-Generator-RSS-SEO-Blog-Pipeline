# I built Roomzine: one photo of your room becomes a magazine-style redesign brief

## Overview

I'm Alexey, a solo developer, and I built Roomzine . You upload one photo of a room as it looks today, pick a style and get a redesign in about 30 seconds. A single AI render is hard to act on: you see a nice picture and still don't know what changed or what to buy. So each result comes out as a magazine spread: before and after, side by side; close-ups of the specific changes (a new headboard, a different tap); the colour palette of the new room; a list of every change and a shopping list; a short editorial text about the room. The full issue is a print-ready PDF. You can hand it to a contractor as a brief or keep it while you plan a renovation. Who it is for People planning a renovation who want to compare a few styles before spending money. Landlords and agents who need virtual staging for a listing. Anyone with a small flat who wonders what it could look like. Styles include Japandi, Scandinavian, Modern Tropical, Mid-century Modern, Bohemian, Industrial, Coastal and Minimalist. What is free Two redesigns are free, with no signup and no card. The full PDF magazine is paid: packs start at $5 for 2 rooms. What I would like feedback on Rooms where the redesign doesn't match your real space (wrong layout, a window moved). This is the failure I care about most. Whether a printable brief is what you want from an AI redesign, or a single image is enough. You can try it here: roomzine.com . I'll answer questions in the comments.
