# Long-Running AI Jobs: Webhooks You Can Trust

## Overview

This job finished, and nobody will ever know. A customer asked for a 40-page report, and the model took six minutes. When it finished, your webhook endpoint was in the middle of a deploy and answered 503. The provider retried for about a minute, then gave up. An hour later, the output was deleted. In this article we build long-running AI jobs that never lose a result: a webhook endpoint you can trust, a worker that applies each result exactly once, and a reconciler that catches what the webhooks miss. Waiting for a slow model A model call that takes minutes can't live inside a request. Hold the connection open, and something in between times out. Poll every two seconds, and you burn your rate limit on jobs that aren't done. So you give the provider a webhook URL, and it calls you when the job finishes. But a webhook is a push with no guarantee. It can arrive twice, late, out of order, or never. Process this webhook? Four webhooks arrive for the same job. Would you process each one, or not?
