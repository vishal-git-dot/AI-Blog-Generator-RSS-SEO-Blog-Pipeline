# What AI Certifications Can Be Renewed Through Continuing Education Credits?

## Overview

Artificial intelligence is evolving rapidly, making continuous learning essential for professionals who want to keep their skills relevant. As AI tools, technologies, and business applications change, many certification programs incorporate continuing education credits or professional development activities to encourage ongoing learning. But which AI certifications support continuing education, and how does the process work? Understanding Continuing Education Credits Continuing education credits may be provided as CPD hours, CEUs, or PDUs, depending on the certification provider. Professionals can typically earn these credits through courses, webinars, workshops, conferences, and other approved learning activities. However, it is important to understand that earning CPD credits does not always mean that a certification is automatically renewed. Each provider has its own maintenance and renewal policies. AI Certifications With Continuing Education Options Future Skills Academy offers AI certifications that include CPD-accredited learning opportunities. For professionals looking to specialize in emerging areas of AI, certifications such as the Certified AI Professional (CAIP) and Certified ChatGPT Professional (CCGP) can provide structured learning and professional development. Specialized AI credentials can also be useful for professionals interested in areas such as AI product management, AI agents, security, and prompt engineering. CPD hours can provide evidence of continued professional learning and may support an individual's broader professional development requirements. Microsoft takes a different approach for eligible AI and cloud certifications. Rather than requiring professionals to retake the original exam, Microsoft provides free online renewal assessments through Microsoft Learn for certifications that qualify for its renewal program. AWS also provides structured recertification pathways for its cloud and machine learning certifications. Depending on the credential, professionals may renew by earning a higher-level certification or completing the applicable recertification requirements. Why Continuing Education Matters AI professionals cannot rely solely on knowledge gained when they first earned a certification. Continuous learning helps them stay informed about new AI models, tools, security practices, regulations, and business applications. A certification that incorporates professional development can make ongoing learning more manageable while helping professionals demonstrate their commitment to keeping their skills current. Final Thoughts If you are comparing AI certifications , look beyond the initial credential. Check the validity period, CPD or continuing education requirements, renewal process, associated fees, and accreditation. Whether you choose a certification from Future Skills Academy, Microsoft, AWS, or another recognized provider, ongoing education can help ensure that your professional knowledge remains relevant as the AI industry continues to evolve.
