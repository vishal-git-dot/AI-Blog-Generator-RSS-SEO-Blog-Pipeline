How Is AI Improving Quality Control in OEM Manufacturing? AI is enhancing quality control in OEM manufacturing by facilitating instant defect detection, automating inspections, and providing intelligent quality monitoring. Conflicting with traditional inspection methods that mostly depend on manual checks and sample testing, AI is always analyzing production data and product images to find defects in a fast and accurate manner.
