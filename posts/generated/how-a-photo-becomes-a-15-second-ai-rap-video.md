For rap, the useful traits are a steady pocket and an ending that lands with the last bar. Fifteen seconds holds an intro plus one musical idea. When the vocal ends, the bed should end with it. Vocals. A speech or singing model performs the lyric. Rap depends on stress and on lines that fit the bar. Typical systems generate audio with timing already in it, or they generate audio and then align it to the beat. Rushed line endings, or a voice sitting beside the snare, come from this stage. Revise the text or regenerate the vocal. The portrait step performs what the audio already says. Animation. A still contains no recorded motion. Lip-sync and portrait models predict a short movement field — jaw, lips, blinks, a little head motion — driven by the audio. One open face is the straightforward case. A crowd, a covered mouth, or a sharp profile gives the tracker less signal. Two photos usually become two subjects or two shots. Mux.
