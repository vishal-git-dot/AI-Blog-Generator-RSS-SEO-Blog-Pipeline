Some of the areas I’m working on: Improving image implementation and consistency across the site Better semantic HTML and page structure Internal linking and contextual relevance Structured data/schema Making service and location information easier for search engines and LLMs to understand Improving Core Web Vitals and overall performance Strengthening entity relationships and local relevance Making content more useful for both traditional search and AI-powered search The interesting part is that optimizing for Google and optimizing for AI systems aren't necessarily the same thing.
