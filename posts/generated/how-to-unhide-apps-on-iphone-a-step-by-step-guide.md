# How to Unhide Apps on iPhone: A Step-by-Step Guide

## Overview

Have you ever searched your iPhone for an app and found that its icon was missing? It can be confusing, especially when you know the app was installed previously. The good news is that a missing app isn't always deleted. It may be hidden, removed from the Home Screen, placed on a hidden page, restricted by Screen Time, or waiting to be downloaded again. In this guide, you'll learn how to find and unhide apps on your iPhone using simple steps. Why Is an App Missing from Your iPhone? Before changing any settings, it's useful to understand what might have happened. Common reasons include: The app was removed from the Home Screen. The entire Home Screen page was hidden. The app was hidden using Apple's app privacy feature. Screen Time restrictions made an app unavailable. The app was deleted or offloaded. The app was hidden from App Store purchase history. Each situation has a different solution, so start with the method that best matches your problem. Method 1: Find an App in the App Library If an app is installed but no longer appears on your Home Screen, check the App Library first. Steps Go to your iPhone's Home Screen. Swipe left past all Home Screen pages. Continue until you reach the App Library. Find the app by browsing the categories or using the App Library search. Touch and hold the app icon. If the option is available, drag the app to your preferred Home Screen page. This method works when an app has been removed from the Home Screen but remains installed. Important: Apps hidden with Apple's dedicated Hidden feature in iOS 18 and later generally won't appear in normal Search results or the usual App Library categories. For those apps, use Method 2. Method 2: Unhide an App from the Hidden Folder Apple introduced a dedicated app-hiding feature in iOS 18. When you hide a compatible app, it moves to the Hidden folder in the App Library and requires authentication to access. Step-by-step instructions Step 1: Open your iPhone's Home Screen. Step 2: Swipe left past all your Home Screen pages to reach the App Library. Step 3: Scroll down to the Hidden folder. Step 4: Tap the folder and authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your iPhone passcode. Step 5: Touch and hold the app you want to restore. Step 6: Select Don't Require Face ID or the equivalent option shown on your device, then authenticate if requested. The app is no longer hidden and appears in the App Library. If you want its icon on your Home Screen, you can move it there. You can also check Settings → Apps → Hidden Apps on supported iOS versions to view the list of hidden apps after authentication. Method 3: Unhide a Home Screen Page If multiple app icons disappeared at once, you may have hidden a whole Home Screen page. How to restore it Touch and hold an empty area on the Home Screen. Tap Edit. Choose Edit Pages, or tap the dots near the bottom of the screen. Look for pages with unchecked circles. Tap the circle for the page you want to display. Tap Done. The page and its apps should become visible again. This method is especially useful when you remember having several apps in one place but can't find any of them on your current Home Screen. Method 4: Check Screen Time Settings Screen Time can restrict access to certain apps and features. This is worth checking if a built-in app, such as Safari, has disappeared or become unavailable. Steps to follow Open Settings. Tap Screen Time. Select Content & Privacy Restrictions. Review the available app, feature, and content restrictions. Adjust the relevant setting if you have permission to do so. The exact options depend on your iOS version and configuration. If the iPhone belongs to a child or is managed by a school or organization, you may need the authorized parent or administrator to make the change. Method 5: Restore an App from the App Store If an app was deleted, you may need to reinstall it. If you previously hid it from your App Store purchase history, you may need to unhide the purchase first. What to do Open the App Store. Tap your account picture. Open your Apple Account settings and look for Hidden Purchases, where available. Unhide the relevant purchase. Return to the App Store and download the app if necessary. Use the Apple Account associated with the original purchase. An app may also be unavailable if it has been removed from the store or no longer supports your device. Unhiding an App Store purchase and unhiding an app on your device are two different actions. Open the folder directly. Resetting the iPhone too soon: Check the App Library, hidden pages, and restrictions before considering a reset. Confusing purchase hiding with app hiding: Hiding an App Store purchase doesn't automatically hide the installed app, and unhiding a purchase doesn't necessarily reinstall it. Changing restrictions without permission: If a device is managed by a parent or organization, follow the appropriate approval process. Frequently Asked Questions Can I unhide apps without deleting them? Yes. If an app is hidden or simply removed from the Home Screen, you can usually restore its visibility without deleting it. Why can't I find a hidden app using Search? When an app is hidden through Apple's privacy feature, it is excluded from normal Search results. Open the Hidden folder in the App Library instead. Will unhiding an app put it back on the Home Screen? Removing an app's hidden status makes it available in the App Library. You may still need to move its icon to the Home Screen. Do I need to reset my iPhone to restore missing apps? Usually not. Try the methods above first. A reset is not the normal solution for a hidden app. Conclusion Learning how to unhide apps on iPhone can save you time and help you avoid unnecessary troubleshooting. Start by checking the App Library, then look in the Hidden folder, restore any hidden Home Screen pages, review Screen Time, and reinstall the app only if needed.
