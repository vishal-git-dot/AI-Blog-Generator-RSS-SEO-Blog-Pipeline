# Futures traders on DEV: send me a month of your NinjaTrader export and I will send back three numbers, free

## Overview

A small open call, and a small experiment. I built an open-source tool for my partner that reads a NinjaTrader trade export and tells her what her own history says: which New York session made her money, what her first five trades of the day cost her, and what re-entering within 15 minutes of a loss cost her. On her account those three were +$1,167 vs +$8 by session, -$327 vs +$2,160 by trade-of-day, and -$179 vs +$1,396 by re-entry timing. She had felt all three and known none of the numbers. I'd like to know how typical that is, and the only way to find out is more accounts. So: The offer. Email a month (or more) of your trade export to aipossiblee@gmail.com with the subject "Three numbers". NinjaTrader 8's Trade Performance export is native; any broker CSV with one row per trade works. Within 48 hours you get those three tables back, computed on your fills. No report, no call, no follow-up unless you ask. Your file is deleted after the reply. The experiment. In two weeks I'll post the aggregate, anonymized: how many accounts had a losing first-five, how many had a re-entry penalty, how spread the session effect was. No individual numbers, no names, and if fewer than five people send anything I'll say so instead of pretending. What this isn't. Advice, signals, or a prediction. It's a description of what you did, in your own numbers.
