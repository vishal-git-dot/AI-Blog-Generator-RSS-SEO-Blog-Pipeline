# Show HN: Foolscap – a writing app that reads your book and never writes for you

## Overview

People are scared of their ability to write and think atrophying, so I wondered: what if I built something that enhanced human creativity instead of destroying it by doing the thinking and writing for people? Foolscap is the result, or at least the first version of that idea that's fleshed out enough to share here. It reads as you write and picks out the structure: characters, ideas, threads, what's left hanging. You can respond to what it picks out, so there's a dialectic between the system and you as the writer. There's also an agent on the side, plugged into the same system, so you can ask about your book and fire off actions through conversation. It never writes into your work. The system can also follow theorems and proofs and string together their dependencies, but I haven't made that a focus yet. There's no Lean support, for example. If anyone's interested in that, I'd love to hear it. I want to build this by iterating with the people who use it: a tool that uses AI not to bypass the creative process, but to bring new tools to it. There's a free tier, and if you email hello@foolscap.works I'll send you a code for 50% off. This is my first product, so feedback of any kind is welcome.
