In practice, the date that matters for you is not 2027. It is the day your own paid plan ends: after it, you lose the paid features and work inside free-plan limits. Before you start, decide what is worth keeping. Most teams do not need every old recording. They need: The list of events and funnels you defined, so you can rebuild them in the new tool. Reports you shared with clients or your team, as files. The few recordings that explain a known bug or a decision, saved with a note on why they matter. Old behavior data ages fast. A redesign, a new theme or a new checkout makes last year's heatmaps less useful. Your event and funnel definitions keep their value, so export those first. Checklist to choose a Smartlook alternative Feature lists look alike, so compare replacements on the points that change your daily work and your costs. Ask each vendor these five questions and write the answers in one table. 1. Where is the data stored? A hosted service keeps recordings on the vendor's servers. A self-hosted plugin keeps them in your own WordPress database and files. This decides who you list as a processor in your privacy policy, how long data is kept, and what happens to it if the vendor changes its offer, as Smartlook customers are seeing now. 2. What does the free quota really cover? Check what is counted (sessions, page views, recordings), whether bots count, and what happens at the limit: does recording pause, does old data disappear, do features lock? A quota that sounds large can be used up by bot traffic on a small site. 3. How long is the trial, and does it need a card? Two weeks rarely covers a full buying cycle. A longer trial lets you see weekend traffic, a campaign and a few real sales before you decide. 4. Does it understand WooCommerce? If you sell with WooCommerce, check that the tool sees the steps product, cart, checkout and order without custom code, and that it can show where buyers leave. 5. What does it cost in page speed? Do not trust a vendor's speed claim, ours included. Measure it: run PageSpeed Insights on the same page three times with the tool on, then three times with it off, and compare the median.
