# Substack Introduces AI Detection Tool for Newsletters

## Overview

Substack has introduced a new AI detection tool for newsletters that helps readers understand whether content was written by a human, generated with AI, or created using a mix of both. Instead of discouraging AI-assisted writing, the platform is focusing on transparency by giving readers more insight into the content they consume. The tool can analyze newsletters, posts, comments, and replies that meet the required character limit. Alongside AI detection, Substack has also launched an optional AI disclosure feature, allowing writers to explain how AI was used during the content creation process. This approach encourages openness while helping creators build stronger trust with their audience. As AI-generated content continues to grow across online platforms, features like these make it easier for readers to understand how content is created. Substack's latest update reflects the increasing demand for transparency and responsible AI use in digital publishing.
