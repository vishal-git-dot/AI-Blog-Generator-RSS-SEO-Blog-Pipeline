Today we examine what happens when mathematical formulas are flattened into raw character strings.* When building retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems over scientific preprints, financial risk whitepapers, or engineering specifications, mathematical queries exhibit the lowest recall in the entire corpus. A user submits a precise query: "What is the scaling factor in equation 4 for policy entropy regularization?" The retrieval model returns unrelated descriptive paragraphs, or the generator produces hallucinated coefficients. The root failure does not originate in the vector store or the embedding dimensionality. It occurs at the document ingestion boundary: converting two-dimensional mathematical notation into a one-dimensional character stream strips the semantic hierarchy of equations, leaving downstream embedding models with disjointed character fragments. How Untagged PDFs Render Mathematical Equations Standard PDFs lack semantic tags for mathematical expressions unless generated under specialized PDF/UA profiles. Instead, a formula is assembled using two separate rendering layers: Font Elevation and Micro-Offsets : Subscripts and superscripts do not carry explicit exponent markers (such as ^ or _ ). Instead, the PDF engine shifts the cursor vertically via text matrix offsets ( Tm ) and reduces font size ( Tf ). A superscript 2 in $x^2$ is simply a small digit placed at an elevated Y-coordinate. Vector Graphic Connectors : Horizontal fraction bars, square root radicals, and grouping brackets are frequently drawn as vector graphic paths ( m , l , re , or curved Bézier strokes) rather than font characters. Because standard text extractors scan only text operators ( Tj , TJ ) and ignore vector drawing paths, fraction bars disappear entirely. The numerator and denominator collapse into adjacent lines of text without operational delimiters. The Three Failure Modes of Naive Formula Extraction When naive text extraction processes mathematical blocks, three distinct structural corruptions destroy embedding vectors: 1. Subscript and Superscript Flattening Because font elevation deltas are discarded during plain text output, superscripts fall onto the baseline. The mathematical expression $e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}$ degrades into e - (x - μ) 2 / 2 σ 2 . The exponent 2 becomes an ordinary multiplicative constant, changing the mathematical semantics completely. 2. Disconnected Fraction and Radical Paths When fraction bars are rendered as vector strokes, naive parsers see the numerator on line $N$ and the denominator on line $N+1$. If a column break or paragraph margin coincides with the equation, the denominator joins the following body paragraph, creating orphan numbers in downstream chunks. 3. Chunk Window Boundary Slicing Standard recursive character splitters treat math punctuation ( = , + , / ) as arbitrary text dividers. Splitting a 4-line display derivation at token 512 leaves variable definitions in chunk A and the resulting differential relation in chunk B. Neither chunk retains sufficient context for standalone vector matching.
