# How Technology Is Reshaping Premium Travel: Dr. Mickael Mosse at the Emirates Skywards Workshop

## Overview

The travel industry is increasingly becoming a technology industry. From AI-powered personalisation and digital loyalty programmes to fintech integrations and premium membership platforms, technology is changing how travellers discover, book, experience, and remember their journeys. Dr. Mickael Mosse, Group CEO and Founder of My NEO Group, recently participated in an exclusive Emirates Skywards workshop focused on the future of loyalty and customer engagement. The discussions explored how modern travel brands can respond to changing customer expectations in an increasingly digital environment. Why Loyalty Is Becoming a Technology Problem Traditional loyalty models are relatively straightforward: Purchase → Earn → Reward Modern loyalty is much more complex. Customers expect brands to understand their preferences, provide personalised experiences, and deliver relevant value across multiple channels. This introduces several technology challenges: Personalisation Customer data Digital identity AI-driven recommendations Cross-platform integrations Real-time engagement Secure digital transactions The future of loyalty therefore depends heavily on the underlying technology infrastructure. Emirates Skywards and NEO Pass During the visit, Dr. Mosse also discussed potential synergies between Emirates Skywards and NEO Pass. NEO Pass is My NEO Group's premium membership ecosystem connecting travel, lifestyle, and fintech services. The potential intersection between these models demonstrates an important industry trend: travel platforms are increasingly becoming broader digital ecosystems. Instead of treating an airline booking as an isolated transaction, technology can connect the traveller with a broader network of services. Technology Inside the A350 Experience Dr. Mosse was also given an exclusive preview of the Emirates Airbus A350 full-scale cabin mock-up. The experience demonstrated how technology and design are increasingly integrated into the passenger journey. The next generation of aircraft cabins is not simply about physical design. It is about creating an environment where comfort, connectivity, personalisation, and digital services can work together. The Future of Connected Travel The most interesting development in premium travel may therefore happen outside the aircraft itself. The real opportunity lies in connecting: Airlines Hotels Fintech AI Loyalty Lifestyle Membership Platforms When these systems communicate effectively, the customer journey can become significantly more personalised and efficient. This is one of the areas where emerging technologies can create meaningful value for the travel industry. Final Thoughts The Emirates Skywards workshop provided an important opportunity to discuss the evolution of travel, loyalty, and digital customer experiences. For My NEO Group, the engagement reinforces a broader vision of connecting technology, fintech, AI, hospitality, luxury travel, and premium membership services. The future traveller will expect more than transportation. They will expect an intelligent ecosystem. The future of travel is connected, personalised, and increasingly digital.
