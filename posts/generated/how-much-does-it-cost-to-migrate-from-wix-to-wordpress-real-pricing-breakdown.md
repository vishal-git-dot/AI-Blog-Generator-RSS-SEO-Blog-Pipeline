# How Much Does It Cost to Migrate from Wix to WordPress? (Real Pricing Breakdown)

## Overview

If your Wix site has outgrown its plan — slow load times, subscription costs creeping up, or design limits you keep bumping into — you're probably wondering what it actually costs to move to WordPress. The honest answer: it depends on how much of your site needs to be rebuilt by hand, because Wix doesn't give you a clean export. Why It Isn't a Simple Copy-Paste Wix locks content into a proprietary system with no built-in export tool, and its layouts rely on absolute positioning — elements placed at exact pixel coordinates. WordPress themes work the opposite way: content flows in a responsive structure. That mismatch means someone has to manually rebuild pages, recreate the design responsively, move content over, and set up redirects. What Actually Drives Cost Page count — the biggest single driver eCommerce vs brochure — Wix Stores → WooCommerce means rebuilding products, categories, shipping, payment gateway Custom animations/interactive elements — Wix's proprietary interactions don't transfer directly SEO/redirect complexity — sites with existing rankings need careful URL mapping Protecting SEO During Migration Map every URL before starting 301 redirect every old URL — skip this and Google treats your new pages as brand new content Preserve meta titles/descriptions, improve later Resubmit sitemap in Google Search Console immediately after launch Check mobile responsiveness on every page How Long Does It Take? Small sites (5-15 pages): about a week with a developer. WooCommerce migrations: 2-3 weeks depending on catalog size. FAQ Will I lose my Google rankings? Not if done correctly — proper redirects and URL mapping mean most sites stabilize within a few weeks. Can I do it myself? Yes, if your site is simple. Plugins move content, but you'll still rebuild the design manually. What happens to my domain? It stays yours — you just point DNS to new hosting instead of Wix's servers.
