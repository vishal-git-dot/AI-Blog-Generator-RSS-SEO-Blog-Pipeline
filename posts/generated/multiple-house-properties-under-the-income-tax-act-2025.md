# Multiple House Properties Under The Income-tax Act 2025

## Overview

Owning than one house has become very common these days. You may have one house where you live with your family another house that you bought as an investment and maybe a third house that you got from your parents or grandparents. Some people buy a house in another city because of their job while others buy extra houses to earn money from rent or to build wealth over time. Even though owning properties can be a good financial decision it also means you have more tax responsibilities. Many people think that all the houses they own are treated the way under the Income-tax Act, 2025. Others think that if a house is empty or only used sometimes it does not affect their taxes. These ideas can lead to mistakes when you file your Income Tax Return.
