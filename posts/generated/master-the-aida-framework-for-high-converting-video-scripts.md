# Master the AIDA Framework for High-Converting Video Scripts

## Overview

Ever wonder why some tech channels rake in millions of views on basic reviews while your deep-dive specs breakdown gets crickets? It’s rarely about your camera gear or B-roll. It’s about narrative structure. Enter the AIDA Model —a classic copywriting framework engineered to keep eyes glued to your screen and fix dying retention graphs. Here is how to map it to your next tech video: 1. Attention (The Hook) You have three seconds before a viewer clicks away. Skip the 3D channel logo and custom intro music. Jump straight into a bold claim, visual contrast, or high-stakes problem. Tech Example: "This $50 gadget just made my $2,000 studio setup completely obsolete." 2. Interest (The Payoff) Now that you have them, feed their curiosity. Layer in intriguing specs, unexpected benchmark stats, or a shared creator frustration to prove you aren't clickbaiting. Tech Example: "I put it through two weeks of heavy 8K rendering alongside the M-Series Max chip. The thermal results made zero sense." 3. Desire (The Transformation) Shift from raw specs to the emotional outcome. Show them how this hardware or software solves a painful problem or upgrades their daily workflow. Tech Example: "Imagine exporting your timeline in half the time without hearing your laptop fans scream. That’s what upgrading this single setting actually feels like." 4. Action (The Next Step) Never end a video with a weak, passive sign-off. Give a direct, high-value command while engagement is at its peak. Tech Example: "Drop your current GPU in the comments, and I'll tell you if this upgrade is worth your money." AIDA works because it mirrors human curiosity.
