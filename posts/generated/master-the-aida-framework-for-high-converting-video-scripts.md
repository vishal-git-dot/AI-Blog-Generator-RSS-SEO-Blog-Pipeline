# Master the AIDA Framework for High-Converting Video Scripts

## Overview

Retention tanking after the first 30 seconds? You’re not alone. Most tech creators spend weeks testing gear, only to lose viewers because the script drags. Enter the AIDA Model —the battle-tested copywriting framework re-engineered to keep eyeballs glued to your videos. Here’s how to use it to skyrocket your retention: 1. Attention (0–5 Seconds) Stop the scroll immediately. Skip the corporate intro and "Hey guys, welcome back." Lead with a bold statement, a visual flex, or a high-stakes question. Tech Example: "This $50 eBay gadget outperforms a $1,200 MacBook—and Apple is not happy about it." 2. Interest (5–30 Seconds) You have their eyes; now grab their minds. Deepen the intrigue by teasing a problem, an absurd spec, or a hidden trick. Tech Example: "It all comes down to a forgotten microchip architecture from 2018 that tech giants deliberately buried." 3. Desire (The Meat) Bridge the gap between specs and emotion. Show the tangible payoff so viewers desperately want the solution, setup, or workflow you’re showcasing. Tech Example: "Imagine rendering 8K timeline footage in under three minutes without hearing a single cooling fan spin up." 4. Action (The Outro) Don't let the video just fade away. Direct your viewer's momentum toward one clear next step while retention is high. Tech Example: "If you want my exact BIOS settings for this build, click the video on screen right now." Structuring your content with AIDA cuts the fluff, maintains high pacing, and turns casual scrollers into loyal subscribers. Stop guessing your script layout—start pacing for retention.
