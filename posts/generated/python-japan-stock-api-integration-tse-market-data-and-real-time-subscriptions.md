# Python Japan Stock API Integration: TSE Market Data and Real-Time Subscriptions

## Overview

More and more friends are doing Asia-market quant work these days, and Japan is one you can't ignore — the TSE Prime (formerly the First Section) has over 2,000 listed companies and ranks in the global top three for average daily turnover. Compared with the US and Hong Kong markets, Japan's code system and trading rules are quite different, and it's easy to trip up when connecting to the data. This post documents the full process of connecting to Japanese stock market data, focusing on Japan's code system and how it differs from other markets. Basics of the Japanese Market Japan's main exchange is the Tokyo Stock Exchange (TSE), which now has three segments: Prime (main board), Standard, and Growth. The Nikkei 225 index and the TOPIX (Tokyo Stock Price Index) you commonly hear about are both selected from the Prime segment. Compared with other markets, the Japanese market has a few characteristics: First, codes are 4-digit numbers. Unlike the US market's alphabetic codes (AAPL) or Hong Kong's 5-digit numeric codes (00700), Japanese stock codes are 4 digits. For example, 7203 is Toyota, 9984 is SoftBank, and 6758 is Sony. Second, trading hours follow Japan Standard Time (JST). JST is 1 hour ahead of Beijing time. The exchange opens at Tokyo time 9:00-11:30 (before noon) and 12:30-15:00 (after noon). Watch out for timezone conversion when doing real-time monitoring. Third, the region code is JP . It's not like US or HK — for the Japanese market you pass JP .
