# How Much Does a Law Firm WordPress Website Cost? (Real Pricing Breakdown)

## Overview

Law firm website quotes are all over the place — one freelancer quotes $300, one agency quotes $25,000. Both can be legitimate, depending on scope. Here's what actually drives the price. What Determines the Cost Number of dedicated practice area pages (not one page covering everything) Attorney bio pages — one per attorney, not a shared team page Design customization level (template vs. fully custom) Intake tools — a basic form vs. an integrated booking/portal system Content writing (many quotes assume you supply the text) Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA) — genuinely important given law firms' accessibility-lawsuit exposure Pricing by Tier Tier Typical Cost Best For Starter (Template) $800 – $2,500 Solo attorneys, new practices Standard (Semi-Custom) $2,500 – $7,000 Small firms, multiple practice areas Advanced/Custom $7,000 – $15,000+ Multi-attorney firms, client portals Enterprise/Agency $15,000 – $50,000+ Large firms, heavy brand needs Ongoing Costs to Budget For Managed hosting: $25–$100/month Maintenance & security: $50–$250/month Local SEO/content (optional): $300–$2,000+/month Get It Right From the Start The biggest cost driver isn't design polish — it's structure. Dedicated pages per practice area, real attorney bios, and basic on-page SEO from day one consistently outperform a visually polished but generically-built site. A recent rebuild for a small family law practice — four practice area pages, two attorney bios, a simplified intake form — came in under $3,500 and started generating inquiries within two months of indexing. FAQs Is WordPress good for law firm sites? Yes — full content ownership, strong SEO flexibility, no platform lock-in. Do I need a custom design? Not usually. A well-customized legal theme covers most small firms. What do firms forget to budget for? Content writing — most quotes assume you're supplying the text. Does the site need WCAG compliance? Strongly recommended given rising ADA accessibility claims against law firms.
