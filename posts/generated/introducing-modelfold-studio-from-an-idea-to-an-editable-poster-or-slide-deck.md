# Introducing ModelFold Studio: From an Idea to an Editable Poster or Slide Deck

## Overview

You have the outline for a presentation, but the first slide is still blank. Or an event is coming up and you need a poster today. Getting to a usable first draft can take longer than expected. We built ModelFold Studio to help with that step. Start with a template or describe what you need, then keep working on the text and layout in the editor. Studio supports individual designs and complete slide decks. This is a walkthrough of our own product. The screenshots show the Chinese interface used in our hands-on test; the English text below explains the same workflow. The example edits and exports were made from a template, without paid AI generation. Start with a template and make it yours Open the template gallery , choose a design, and sign in to create your own editable copy. For a quarterly review, the business presentation template gives you a starting layout. Replace the title, reporting period, and key points, then adjust the placement of individual elements. In our test, we opened this template in the editor, selected the main title in the layer panel, and changed it to the Chinese equivalent of “Q4 Business Review.” We also updated the subtitle to describe the team's results and next steps. Changes save automatically. The layer panel helps you select the element you want. The canvas shows the design, and the properties panel lets you change its text and appearance. Preparing a product announcement instead? The product launch template offers another starting point. Describe the first draft you need When you want to start from a prompt, give Studio the audience, format, required content, and visual direction. For example: Create a portrait opening-week poster for Mountain Coffee. Use warm cream and coffee-brown colors with a clean, welcoming layout. Make “Opening Week: Second Drink Half Price” the headline. Include November 1–7 and the sample address 18 Example Street. The design will be used as an Instagram feed post. Choose an appropriate canvas size, enter the brief, and generate a draft. Pick a result and continue editing it: refine the headline, correct the offer details, or move an element to improve the layout. The same approach works for posters, social media covers, infographics, and individual presentation pages. Custom canvas dimensions are available when you have a specific size requirement. For a full deck, review the outline first Switch to the full slide deck mode, enter your topic, and choose the slide count, language, and visual theme. Generate an outline before creating the pages. This gives you a chance to review slide titles, key points, and order while changes are still quick to make. For an employee onboarding deck, for example, check that the outline covers the team, everyday workflows, and common questions before generating the slides. Once the pages are ready, continue editing them individually and use presentation mode to preview the flow. Review the story before polishing the visual details. Export for the next step Studio offers PNG, JPG, PDF, and PPTX exports, plus a PNG ZIP option for multi-page designs. We downloaded both PNG and PPTX files during the template walkthrough.
